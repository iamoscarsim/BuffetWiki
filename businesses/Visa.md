# Visa

The global payments network, held as a marketable equity first itemized in the [[2019]] portfolio at
**10.2M shares (0.6% of Visa), cost $349M / market $1,924M** — a small but highly profitable position of
the kind run by **[[Todd Combs]]** and **[[Ted Weschler]]**. Visa is a quintessential **toll-bridge /
[[Economic Moat|wide-moat]]** franchise (a duopoly with Mastercard, capital-light, high-margin) — the same
"royalty on commerce" quality Buffett prizes in [[Moody's]] and [[American Express]].

## Evolution
- [[2019]] New itemized holding: 0.6% of Visa, ~$349M cost (already nearly 6× in market value).
