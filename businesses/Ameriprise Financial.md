# Ameriprise Financial

A financial-planning and asset-management company that entered Berkshire's portfolio in 2005
([[2005]]) when **[[American Express]] spun it off** (Berkshire received 30,322,137 shares, ~12.1%,
market $1,243M). Like the simultaneous Gillette→[[Procter & Gamble]] conversion, it was a holding
acquired by corporate action rather than purchase. Berkshire exited the position over the following
couple of years.

## Evolution
- [2005] Received via the American Express spin-off (~12.1%, $1.2B market); later sold.
