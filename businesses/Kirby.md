# Kirby

Kirby makes premium home-cleaning (vacuum) systems and is, with [[World Book]], one
of the two flagship units of [[Scott Fetzer]], acquired by Berkshire after year-end
1985. It is a direct-sales business with a durable, quality-driven franchise.

In the [[1986]] letter Kirby recorded its fourth straight year of unit-volume gains —
worldwide units **+4% over 1985 and +33% over 1982**. Buffett frames the product as a
premium good that earns its price: "more expensive than most cleaners, it performs in
a manner that leaves cheaper units far behind ('in the dust,' so to speak). Many 30-
and 40-year-old Kirby cleaners are still in active duty. If you want the best, you buy
a Kirby." Like [[World Book]], Kirby is a direct-selling organization that "responded
most successfully" to the new challenges of the working-woman era when other direct
sellers stumbled.

## Evolution
- [1985] Joined Berkshire inside [[Scott Fetzer]] as one of its 17 businesses.
- [1986] Worldwide units +4% (and +33% over 1982); the durable premium-vacuum
  franchise ("if you want the best, you buy a Kirby").
