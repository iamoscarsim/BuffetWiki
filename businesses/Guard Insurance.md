# Guard Insurance

A Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania **workers'-compensation insurer**, primarily serving smaller
businesses, acquired by Berkshire **late in 2012** as a bolt-on to its "Other Primary" insurance
group ([[2012]]). Annual premiums were about **$300 million** at purchase, with "excellent prospects
for growth in both its traditional business and new lines." Guard joined Berkshire's collection of
smaller specialty insurers that "ply their trade in odd corners of the insurance world" and
collectively provide substantial, consistently profitable [[Insurance Float|float]].

## Evolution
- [2012] Acquired (~$300M annual premiums); a workers'-comp bolt-on to the Other Primary insurance
  segment.
- [[2019]] **"Bingo and bingo."** Buffett devotes a section to GUARD's success: bought by **[[Ajit Jain]]**
  in late 2012 for **$221M** (≈ net worth), with CEO **Sy Foguel** ("going to be a star at Berkshire").
  By 2019, premium volume reached **$1.9 billion — up 379% since 2012** — with a satisfactory underwriting
  profit and float up **265%**, after Foguel pushed into new products and regions. "In 1967, Omaha seemed
  an unlikely launching pad for a P/C giant. **Wilkes-Barre** may well deliver a similar surprise."
