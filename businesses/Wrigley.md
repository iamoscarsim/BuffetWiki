# Wrigley

The chewing-gum maker, acquired by **[[Mars]]** in 2008 in a deal Berkshire helped finance — one of
the three crisis-era fixed-income commitments made "on terms that would be unavailable in normal
markets" (with [[Goldman Sachs]] and [[General Electric]]). Berkshire put **$6.5 billion** into the
Mars/Wrigley transaction: a $4.4B subordinated-note portion (yielding ~11.45%) plus a $2.1B equity
stake in Wrigley ([[2008]]). Like the Goldman and GE deals, it paired a high current yield with an
equity participation "as a bonus."

The connection surfaces in a charming aside: at the 2009 meeting, TTI's [[Paul Andrews Jr|Paul Andrews]] displayed a 1935 Duesenberg "that once belonged to Mrs. Forrest Mars, Sr., parent and
grandparent of our new partners in the Wrigley purchase." The three 2008 commitments totaled
**$14.5 billion**.
