# Constellation Energy

A Baltimore-based electric utility that "was within hours of bankruptcy" in the September 2008 panic
"when [[MidAmerican Energy|MidAmerican]] arrived to effect a rescue" ([[2008]]). MidAmerican agreed
to acquire it, but the deal was ultimately topped by a rival bid and broke up — to Berkshire's
profit. The episode contributed **$1,092 million** to MidAmerican's 2008 earnings: a **$175 million
breakup fee** plus a **$917 million profit** on Berkshire's investment in the company.

Buffett cited Constellation as a real-life illustration of the **collateral-posting "time bomb"**
embedded in derivatives: the firm's near-collapse stemmed from margin/posting requirements moving
against it — the same lethal mechanism he had warned about in the 2002 [[Derivatives]] essay and
deliberately avoided in Berkshire's own derivative book.
