# Applied Underwriters

A company offering a combined **payroll-services and workers'-compensation** package to small
businesses (mostly in California), 81% of which Berkshire agreed to buy in December 2005 ([[2005]]).
Founders **Sid Ferenc** and **Steve Menzies** had built it from a shoestring start 12 years earlier;
in a "brilliant insight," they had concentrated operations in **Omaha** (400 of 479 employees). The
connection to Berkshire came through insurance: Applied had entered a large reinsurance contract with
**[[Ajit Jain]]**, who was impressed by Ferenc and Menzies — "so we decided to join forces." The two
retained **19%**.

## Evolution
- [2005] 81% acquired (via an Ajit Jain reinsurance relationship); Omaha-based payroll + workers'-comp
  for small businesses; founders Sid Ferenc & Steve Menzies keep 19%.
