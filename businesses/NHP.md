# NHP

NHP, Inc. is a developer, syndicator, owner and manager of multi-family rental
housing in which Berkshire bought about **50% for $23.7 million** in 1985, described
in the [[1986]] letter. It is a minor, atypical holding — not a wholly owned
operating business but a large minority stake in a complex housing enterprise.

NHP has an "unusual genealogy": it grew out of a 1967 commission (led by Edgar Kaiser,
appointed by President Johnson) to expand housing for low- and moderate-income
tenants; over 260 corporations invested $42M out of public-service motives. By 1986
NHP oversaw ~500 partnership properties (≈80,000 housing units in 40 states) costing
over $2.5 billion, earning ~$16M a year in management revenue plus residual interests
that "could prove quite valuable, particularly if inflation should heat up." The Tax
Reform Act of 1986 halted the tax-oriented syndication that had driven the original
model, pushing NHP toward equity and residual positions in market-rate properties.
Co-owners included Weyerhaeuser (~25%) and a management group under CEO Rod Heller.
