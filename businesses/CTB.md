# CTB

A worldwide leader in **equipment for the poultry, hog, egg-production and grain industries**,
acquired by Berkshire in 2002 ([[2002]]) — one of two "somewhat below our normal size threshold"
deals that year (with **[[Garan]]**), though together earning more than $60M pre-tax. CTB operates
"in [an] industr[y] characterized by tough economics" but has "important competitive strengths
that enable [it] to earn decent returns on capital." It came with its long-time manager **Vic
Mancinelli**, "responsible for [its] impressive record" — exactly the able-owner-operator Berkshire
seeks ([[Acquisition Criteria]]). CTB became a quietly compounding agricultural-equipment business
within Berkshire's manufacturing group.

## Evolution
- [2002] Acquired (sub-threshold size); agricultural/livestock equipment leader; manager Vic
  Mancinelli.
