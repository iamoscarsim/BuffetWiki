# Southwest Airlines

The low-cost U.S. carrier, bought by Berkshire in [[2016]] as part of its re-entry into the airline
industry ([[Evolving Views — Airlines]]). Disclosed in the [[2016]] portfolio at **43.2M shares (7.0% of
Southwest), cost $1,757M / market $2,153M**. As the original low-cost operator, Southwest fit Buffett's
long-standing preference for the **low-cost producer** in a commodity business — the same edge he prizes
at [[GEICO]] — now applied, cautiously, to an industry he had spent decades disparaging.

## Evolution
- [[2016]] New holding: 7.0% of Southwest, ~$1.8B cost. Part of the all-four-carriers basket bet.
- [[2020]] **Sold in full** during the COVID-19 travel collapse (announced May 2020), at a loss — gone
  from the year-end portfolio. See [[Evolving Views — Airlines]].
