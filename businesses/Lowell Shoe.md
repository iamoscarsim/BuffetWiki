# Lowell Shoe

Lowell Shoe Company is a footwear maker — best known for **Nursemates**, a leading line
of shoes for nurses, along with other styles — with about **$90 million in sales**. It
was acquired at year-end 1992 not by Berkshire's parent but by **[[H. H. Brown]]**, as
the largest of Berkshire's five 1992 **add-on** acquisitions ([[1992]]). Buffett presents
such bolt-ons as "a low-risk and high-return proposition": they "enlarge the domain of
managers we already know to be outstanding" — here, extending [[Frank Rooney]]'s shoe
operation — rather than betting on an unfamiliar management (see [[Acquisition Criteria]]).

## Evolution
- [1992] Acquired by H. H. Brown (~$90M sales; Nursemates) — the largest of five Berkshire
  subsidiary add-on acquisitions in the year.
