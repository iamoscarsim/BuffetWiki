# PacifiCorp

A major **Western U.S. electric utility** serving ~1.6 million customers across **six states**
(Oregon and Utah the largest), acquired by **[[MidAmerican Energy]]** in a deal agreed in 2005 and
closed in 2006 ([[2005]], [[2006]]). The purchase (~$5.1 billion: **$3.4B** of Berkshire-bought
MidAmerican common plus **$1.7B** of MidAmerican borrowing) added **$3.3B of revenue** and **$14.1B
of assets**, roughly doubling MidAmerican's scale and confirming Buffett's stated appetite for
large, repeatable utility deals ("Note the plural — we'll be looking for **more**").

PacifiCorp embodies the regulated-utility logic Buffett embraces: "you can't expect to earn outsized
profits in regulated utilities, but the industry offers owners the opportunity to **deploy large
sums at fair returns**" — exactly what a cash-rich Berkshire needs.

## Evolution
- [2005] Agreed (via MidAmerican); ~1.6M customers, six states; ~$5.1B; the marquee utility deal
  signalling more to come.
- [2006] **Closed** (March 21); adds $3.3B revenue / $14.1B assets to MidAmerican, which now serves
  millions of electric customers across the U.S. and U.K.
