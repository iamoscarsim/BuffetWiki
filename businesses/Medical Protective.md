# Medical Protective (MedPro)

A 106-year-old **medical-malpractice insurer** based in Fort Wayne, acquired by Berkshire on June
30, 2005 ([[2005]]). Malpractice insurance "is tough to underwrite and has proved to be a graveyard
for many insurers," but Buffett judged MedPro a fit for two Berkshire-specific reasons: the cultural
"attitudinal advantage… wherein **underwriting discipline trumps all other goals**," and a balance
sheet "far exceeding that of its competitors" — vital for a long-tail line where doctors need
assurance "that long-to-settle claims will not end up back on their doorstep because their insurer
failed." CEO **Tim Kenesey** "instinctively thinks like a Berkshire manager."

Berkshire **raised MedPro's loss reserves by ~$125M** immediately after purchase — a reminder that
"surprises in insurance are far from symmetrical" (see [[Loss Reserving]]). Excluding that addition
for prior periods, MedPro wrote at an underwriting profit.

## Evolution
- [2005] Acquired (6/30); medical-malpractice insurer with a disciplined culture and Berkshire's
  balance-sheet edge; CEO Tim Kenesey; reserves raised $125M post-purchase.
