# White Mountains Insurance

A property-casualty insurance holding company, a new Berkshire equity position first itemized in
[[2004]] (**1,724,200 shares**, ~16% of the company, cost $369M, market $1,114M). The connection is
personal and historical: White Mountains is the vehicle long associated with **[[Jack Byrne]]** —
the executive who rescued **[[GEICO]]** in 1976 — and Berkshire's investment was effectively a bet
on Byrne's insurance acumen and the disciplined-underwriting culture he embodied. The stake was a
relatively modest, minority financial holding rather than a control position.
