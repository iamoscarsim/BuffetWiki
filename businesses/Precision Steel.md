# Precision Steel

Precision Steel Warehouse, a Chicago-area steel-service center, was acquired
through [[Wesco Financial]] and first appears in Berkshire's Sources of Earnings
table in the [[1979]] letter, contributing $3.25 million pre-tax. A modest but
steady operating business, it is one of the thirteen businesses Buffett notes
Berkshire and its Blue Chip/Wesco subsidiaries had acquired outright from private
owners by 1979.

## Evolution
- [1979] First appears in the earnings table (~$3.25M pre-tax); held via Wesco.
- [1988] Buffett singles out **Dave Hillstrom**'s performance running Precision: in "an
  extremely competitive industry" he "consistently achieves good returns on invested
  capital" — likely ranking "number one among his peers," in 1988 and years past.
