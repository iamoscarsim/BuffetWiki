# Leucadia National

A diversified holding company and Berkshire's partner in the 2001 **Berkadia** joint venture
that funded the **[[FINOVA Group]]** prepackaged bankruptcy ([[2001]]). Leucadia and Berkshire
jointly owned Berkadia, which borrowed $5.6 billion and re-lent it to FINOVA; Berkshire
guaranteed 90% of the borrowing (and held a secondary guarantee on Leucadia's 10%), while
**Leucadia took day-to-day operating responsibility** for FINOVA. Buffett noted he had "long
been impressed with the business acumen and managerial talent of its key executives" — a regard
that would recur in later dealings between the two firms.

## Evolution
- [2001] Berkadia partner on FINOVA; Buffett praises Leucadia's executives.
