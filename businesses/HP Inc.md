# HP Inc.

The personal-computer and printer maker (the hardware company spun out of the old Hewlett-Packard), a
**new 2022 equity position** large enough that the [[2022]] letter lists Berkshire as **the largest owner**
of the company (one of "eight giants" of the S&P 500 Berkshire topped). The stake — a value-priced,
cash-generative, heavily-buying-back business — bears the marks of the bank-and-value style of recent
Berkshire purchases. It proved relatively short-lived: Berkshire trimmed and then largely exited HP over
2023–24.

## Evolution
- [[2022]] New holding; Berkshire became HP Inc.'s largest shareholder. *(Largely sold by 2023–24.)*
