# Marmon Group

The **125-business industrial conglomerate** (organized in nine sectors; largest unit **Union Tank
Car**, which with a Canadian counterpart owns 94,000 leased rail cars; ~$7B sales, ~20,000
employees) built by the **[[Jay Pritzker|Pritzker]]** family, acquired by Berkshire beginning on
**Christmas Day 2007** — "**the largest cash purchase in Berkshire's history**" to that point
([[2007]]). Berkshire bought **60% for $4.5 billion**, with the **remaining ~40% to be acquired
within six years** on an earnings-based formula.

The deal traced to a 1954 friendship: a 24-year-old Buffett, sent by Graham-Newman to a Rockwood
Chocolate meeting, was schooled in tax-efficient dealmaking by **[[Jay Pritzker]]** (whose brother
Bob ran Marmon for ~50 years). After Jay's 1999 death the family decided to sell, and "**a deal is a
deal**" — Buffett priced it off the financial statements, "employing no advisors and engaging in no
nit-picking," and closed amid chaotic markets when "many large deals [were being] renegotiated or
killed." CEO **[[Frank Ptak]]** (with John Nichols, ex-Illinois Tool Works); facilitated by
**[[Byron Trott]]**; code-named "**Indy 500**" (Marmon's Wasp won the first Indianapolis 500 in 1911).

## Evolution
- [2007] 60% acquired for $4.5B (Christmas Day) — Berkshire's largest-ever cash purchase; rest to
  follow within six years; the Pritzker/Jay Pritzker history; CEO Frank Ptak.
- [2008] Performed solidly through the crisis; Berkshire continued the staged purchase of additional
  Marmon equity on the earnings formula.
- [2010] Berkshire moves to **80%** via the scheduled ~$1.5B purchase of 17% from the Pritzker family
  (remaining shares to be bought in 2013 or 2014, at the family's election). Marmon (130 businesses;
  Frank Ptak) is the **largest earner** in the Mfg/Svc/Retail sector.
- [2011] The formula-based purchases proceed; Marmon's diverse industrials a steady earner.
- [2013] Berkshire completed the buy-in to **~100%** of Marmon (the final tranches at formula prices),
  making it a wholly-owned industrial pillar.
