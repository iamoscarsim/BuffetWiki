# Richline Group

A **jewelry manufacturing/supply** company built under Berkshire in 2007 ([[2007]]). Buffett met
**Dennis Ulrich** — owner of a gold-jewelry maker — at a vendor lunch for **[[Ben Bridge Jeweler]]**
in 2006; in January 2007 Ulrich proposed that "with Berkshire's support he could build a large
jewelry supplier." Berkshire bought his business and a roughly equal-sized supplier simultaneously,
combining them into **Richline Group** (with partner Dave Meleski). Buffett admitted Richline was
"far below the earnings threshold we normally require," but bet on Ulrich to "build a large operation,
earning good returns on capital" — an example of backing a proven operator to roll up a fragmented
industry.

## Evolution
- [2007] Formed (Dennis Ulrich + Dave Meleski) from two jewelry-supply acquisitions; below the usual
  size threshold, a bet on the operator.
