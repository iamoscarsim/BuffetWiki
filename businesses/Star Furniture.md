# Star Furniture

Star Furniture is a Houston-based home-furnishings retailer Berkshire acquired in 1997
(closed **July 1**) — the third of Buffett's "**[[Acquisition Criteria|any more at home like you?]]**" furniture chains. The referral chain is the point: when Berkshire bought
**[[Nebraska Furniture Mart]]** in 1983, the Blumkins named three outstanding furniture
retailers elsewhere; one was **[[R.C. Willey Home Furnishings|R.C. Willey]]** (bought
1995); R.C. Willey's [[Bill Child]] then named the remaining two, one being Star.

Run by longtime controlling shareholder and CEO **[[Melvyn Wolff]]** and his sister
**Shirley Toomin**, Star had struggled until the siblings took over in 1962, and by 1997
operated **12 stores** (ten in Houston, plus Austin and Bryan, with San Antonio coming).
The deal was made "in a single, two-hour session" on a handshake of trust — "I knew I was
dealing with a man of integrity and that's what counted." When the Wolffs told their
associates of the sale, they funded large special payments to *everyone* in the business
out of their *own* proceeds: "Charlie and I love it when we become partners with people
who behave like that." Buffett expected Star to be "many times its present size a decade
from now."

## Evolution
- [1997] Acquired (closed 7/1/97); 12 Texas stores; CEO [[Melvyn Wolff]] and Shirley
  Toomin. Sourced through the Blumkin → R.C. Willey ([[Bill Child]]) referral chain;
  folded into the Home Furnishings segment (which earned $56.8M pre-tax with Star from
  July 1). "Already-excellent sales and earnings growth" accelerated post-close.
- [1999] [[Melvyn Wolff]] and his sister Shirley Toomim "scored two major successes: a **move into
  San Antonio** and a significant enlargement of Star's store in **Austin**." Star is part of the
  four-chain furniture group that now makes Berkshire #1 in furniture across NE, UT, ID, TX, MA and
  NH, and it helped lead Buffett to the fourth chain, [[Jordan's Furniture]] (the Tatelmans were the
  standout the Wolffs and others kept naming).
