# JPMorgan Chase

The largest U.S. bank, held as a marketable equity first itemized in the [[2018]] portfolio at **50.7M
shares (1.5% of the company), cost $5,605M / market $4,946M** — a fresh, large position taken during
2018's ~$43B of net equity buying. It joined Berkshire's substantial bank cluster ([[Bank of America]],
[[Wells Fargo]], [[US Bancorp]], [[Bank of New York Mellon]]) and reflected Buffett's long admiration for
CEO Jamie Dimon (whose "what is smart at one price is dumb at another" capital-allocation discipline
Buffett quoted approvingly in [[2011]]). Berkshire would later sell out of JPMorgan around 2020.

## Evolution
- [[2018]] New holding: 1.5% of JPMorgan Chase, ~$5.6B cost.
