# POSCO

The South Korean steelmaker (one of the world's most efficient), a Berkshire equity holding first
itemized in [[2006]] (3.49M shares, ~4.0%, market $1,158M). It exemplifies Buffett's mid-2000s turn
toward **foreign equities** — both as understandable, well-run, low-cost franchises and as a source
of non-dollar earnings as he wound down the direct currency bet (see [[Trade Deficit and the Dollar]]). Buffett repeatedly praised POSCO as a superbly-run company; Berkshire held it for years
before exiting.

## Evolution
- [2006] First itemized (~4.0%, $1.16B) — a best-in-class foreign (Korean) steel franchise.
