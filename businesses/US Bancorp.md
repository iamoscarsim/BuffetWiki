# US Bancorp

The Minneapolis-based regional bank holding company, a Berkshire equity holding first itemized in
[[2006]] (31.0M shares, ~1.8%, market $1,123M) — one of several well-run banks Buffett accumulated
alongside **[[Wells Fargo]]** and **[[M&T Bank]]**, prizing disciplined underwriting and high returns
on assets. Berkshire built the position over subsequent years into one of its larger bank holdings
(eventually exiting it in the early 2020s).

## Evolution
- [2006] First itemized (~1.8%, $1.12B) — a disciplined regional bank franchise.
