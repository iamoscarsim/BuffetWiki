# H&R Block

The U.S. tax-preparation leader, a new equity holding disclosed in [[2001]]
(**15,999,200 shares**, cost $255M, market $715M) — one of two new positions (with
**[[Moody's]]**) added in an otherwise quiet portfolio year. Block fit Buffett's preference
for an understandable business with a strong consumer brand and a large, recurring retail
franchise; at the time the company also had a fast-growing financial-services arm.

## Evolution
- [2001] First disclosed as a >$500M holding (16M shares, market $715M).
