# M&T Bank

The Buffalo-based bank holding company (M&T Bank Corporation), a new equity holding first itemized
in [[2002]] (**6,708,760 shares**, cost $103M, market $532M). The position reflects Berkshire's
long regard for **[[Bob Wilmers]]**, M&T's CEO — the same banker Berkshire had backed in 1991 via
the **[[First Empire State]]** convertible preferred (First Empire being M&T's predecessor name).
Wilmers, whose shareholder letters Buffett admired, ran M&T as a disciplined, conservatively
underwritten franchise bank — and Berkshire held the stake for many years.

## Evolution
- [2002] First itemized as a >$500M holding (6.7M shares); the continuation of Berkshire's
  backing of [[Bob Wilmers]] (ex-[[First Empire State]]).
