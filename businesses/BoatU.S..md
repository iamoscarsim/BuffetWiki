# BoatU.S.

The Boat Owners Association of The United States — which manages an association of about **650,000
boat owners**, providing services (including **boat insurance**) "similar to those offered by AAA
auto clubs to drivers" — added to Berkshire's cluster of smaller specialty insurers in 2007
([[2007]]), headed by **Bill Oakerson**. It fits the Berkshire pattern of niche, well-underwritten
insurance operations that "perform extraordinarily well, earning above-average underwriting profits
and delivering valuable float."

## Evolution
- [2007] Added to the smaller-insurers group (Bill Oakerson); a 650,000-member boat-owners
  association with an insurance arm.
