# Verizon Communications

U.S. telecom giant, a **large new position** in the [[2020]] portfolio: **146.7M shares (3.5% of Verizon),
cost $8,691M / market $8,620M**. The stake fit a 2020 tilt toward steady, dividend-rich, defensive
cash-generators (alongside [[Chevron]] and the pharma names) bought during the COVID year — a high-yield
"bond substitute" at a time when, as Buffett stressed, "bonds are not the place to be" (the 10-year
Treasury yielding 0.93%). It proved a short-lived holding — Berkshire sold most of it during 2021.
