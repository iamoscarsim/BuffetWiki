# Dow Chemical

The U.S. chemical maker. Berkshire bought **$3 billion of Dow Chemical convertible preferred** (an
8.5% dividend) to help fund Dow's 2009 acquisition of Rohm and Haas — another crisis-era
**"unavailable in normal markets"** capital infusion in the spirit of the 2008 [[Goldman Sachs]],
[[General Electric]] and [[Wrigley]] deals ([[2009]]). It is one of the five **non-traded
securities** Buffett groups together that year ([[Dow Chemical]], [[General Electric]], [[Goldman Sachs]], [[Swiss Re]], [[Wrigley]]) — an aggregate cost of $21.1B carried at $26.0B and throwing off
**$2.1 billion a year** in dividends and interest, with equity upside as a bonus. Berkshire sold
holdings (ConocoPhillips, P&G, J&J) early in 2009 partly to raise the cash for the Dow and Swiss Re
purchases.
