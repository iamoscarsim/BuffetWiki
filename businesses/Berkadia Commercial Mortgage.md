# Berkadia Commercial Mortgage

A commercial-mortgage servicer and originator — the **#3 servicer in the country** (a ~$235B
portfolio, 25 offices) — formed at the end of **2009** when Berkshire and **[[Leucadia National]]**
jointly bought the servicing/origination business of the bankrupt **Capmark**, each taking **50%**
([[2009]]). The name and the partnership revive the earlier **Berkadia** venture (the 2001
**[[FINOVA Group]]** distressed-debt workout): "Our first venture was also christened Berkadia. So
let's call this one **Son of Berkadia**. Someday I'll be writing you about Grandson of Berkadia."

The partners are Leucadia's **Joe Steinberg and Ian Cumming**, with whom Buffett had had "a terrific
experience" on FINOVA — "Joe and Ian did far more than their share of the work, an arrangement I
always encourage." Though "commercial real estate will face major problems in the next few years,"
Buffett judged the long-term opportunity significant.

## Evolution
- [2009] Berkshire + [[Leucadia National]] buy Capmark's commercial-mortgage operation 50/50 at
  year-end — "Son of Berkadia"; the #3 U.S. commercial-mortgage servicer (~$235B portfolio).
