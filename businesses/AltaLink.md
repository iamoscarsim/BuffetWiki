# AltaLink

A Canadian **electric-transmission system serving ~85% of Alberta's population**, acquired in **2014
for about C$3 billion** by **[[MidAmerican Energy|Berkshire Hathaway Energy]]** ([[2014]]). It further
diversified BHE's regulated earnings streams (a key source of the utility group's debt-servicing
strength and lower cost of debt) and extended Berkshire's utility footprint beyond the U.S. — part of
Buffett's pledge that BHE "will continue to buy and build utility operations throughout the world for
decades to come."

## Evolution
- [2014] Acquired by BHE for ~C$3B; an Alberta electric-transmission utility (85% of the province),
  broadening BHE's regulatory and geographic diversity.
