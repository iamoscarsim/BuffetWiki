# The Pampered Chef

A direct-sales kitchenware company, the **largest acquisition Berkshire initiated in 2002**
([[2002]]), and one of Buffett's favorite founder stories. **[[Doris Christopher]]**, a 34-year-old
suburban-Chicago home-economics teacher with "absolutely no business background," borrowed
**$3,000 against her life-insurance policy** in 1980 — "all the money ever injected into the
company" — bought a dozen each of useful kitchen items at the Merchandise Mart, and began holding
in-home product demonstrations for small groups of women. First-year sales were $50,000; by 2002,
**22 years later, TPC did more than $700 million** through **67,000 kitchen consultants**.

Christopher had brought in **Sheila O'Connell Cooper** as CEO two years earlier; the two met
Buffett in Omaha in August 2002 and "it took me about ten seconds to decide that these were two
managers with whom I wished to partner." The products are "in large part proprietary," and the
party-plan model gives the consultants an entrepreneurial stake — the kind of motivated,
owner-like workforce Buffett prizes.
