# Alleghany Corporation

A diversified **property-casualty insurer and reinsurer** (its reinsurance arm is TransRe), acquired by
Berkshire in [[2022]] for about **$11.6 billion** in cash — Berkshire's largest acquisition since Precision
Castparts (2016) and a return to its insurance roots. The deal's appeal was classic: Alleghany "delivers
special value… because Berkshire's unmatched **[[Financial Strength|financial strength]]** allows its
insurance subsidiaries to follow valuable and enduring investment strategies unavailable to virtually all
competitors" — i.e., Berkshire can invest Alleghany's float in equities where a stand-alone insurer would
be forced into low-yielding bonds.

It is "captained by **[[Joe Brandon]]**" — the executive who had run **[[General Re]]** through its
post-2001 turnaround before departing in 2008, now back in the Berkshire fold ("I've worked with Joe in
the past, and he understands both Berkshire and insurance"). Alleghany helped lift Berkshire's
**[[Insurance Float|float]] from $147B to $164B** in 2022.

## Evolution
- [[2022]] Acquired (~$11.6B cash); P/C insurer/reinsurer under returning CEO **[[Joe Brandon]]**; boosts
  float toward $164B and adds equity-investable capital.
