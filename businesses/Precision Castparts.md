# Precision Castparts

The world's premier supplier of **aerospace components** (castings, forgings and fasteners, mostly for
original equipment on large aircraft, plus spares), acquired in early 2016 for **more than $32 billion
of cash** — at the time **Berkshire's largest-ever acquisition** ([[2015]]). Announced in the 2015
letter as the coming sixth member of the **"Powerhouse Six,"** PCC "fits perfectly into the Berkshire
model and will substantially increase our normalized per-share earning power."

CEO **[[Mark Donegan]]** "has become the world's premier supplier of aerospace components" — Buffett
likens him to **[[ISCAR|IMC]]'s Jacob Harpaz**: each "transform[s] very ordinary raw materials into
extraordinary products… the **da Vinci of his craft**." PCC has 30,466 employees across 162 plants in
13 countries, sells largely under multi-year contracts, and grows by acquisition (Donegan "has made
many acquisitions and will make more"). The deal was sourced through **[[Todd Combs]]**, who "brought
the company to my attention a few years ago and went on to educate me about both the business and
Mark."

## Evolution
- [2015] Agreed for **>$32B cash** (closed early 2016) — Berkshire's largest acquisition; aerospace-
  components leader under Mark Donegan; the sixth "Powerhouse" earner; sourced via Todd Combs.
- [[2016]] **Closed.** Now part of the Manufacturing, Service & Retailing group; will contribute its
  **first full year of earnings in 2017** (alongside [[Duracell]]). Folded into the group whose units
  earned **24% after-tax on net tangible assets** — though carrying a large goodwill premium reflecting
  the ~$32B price.
- [[2017]] **First full year; grows by acquisition.** PCC bought Germany's **Wilhelm Schulz GmbH** (a
  maker of corrosion-resistant fittings, piping systems and components). Buffett candidly defers to the
  manager: "I don't understand manufacturing operations as well as I do… real estate brokers, home
  builders or truck stops… Fortunately, I don't need… to bring knowledge to the table: **[[Mark Donegan]]**
  is an extraordinary manufacturing executive… **Betting on people can sometimes be more certain than
  betting on physical assets.**" One of the next-five non-insurance earners (with Clayton, IMC, Lubrizol,
  Marmon: $5.5B combined pre-tax). *(PCC would later prove vulnerable to the 2020 aerospace collapse.)*
- [[2020]] **The $11 billion write-down — "I paid too much."** The COVID aerospace collapse forced an
  **~$11B impairment** (the bulk of Berkshire's $11B GAAP write-down for the year), which Buffett owns as
  "**a mistake I made in 2016**": "No one misled me… I was simply too optimistic about PCC's normalized
  profit potential." He still rates it "**a fine company — the best in its business**" with an excellent
  CEO (**[[Mark Donegan]]**); the error was the **price**, not the business — he overestimated average
  future earnings. "**PCC is far from my first error of that sort. But it's a big one.**" (See [[Mistakes Ledger]].)
