# Diversified Retailing Company

Diversified Retailing Company, Inc. was a holding company Buffett and
[[Charlie Munger]] controlled, merged into Berkshire Hathaway on December 30,
**1978** ([[1978]]). The merger's chief consequence was structural: Berkshire's
ownership of [[Blue Chip Stamps]] rose to approximately **58%**, requiring full
consolidation of Blue Chip's businesses into Berkshire's financial statements.
Diversified also brought 100% ownership of [[Associated Retail Stores]] directly
into Berkshire. The merger is a step in the long simplification of the tangled
Buffett–Munger ownership web (Berkshire / Diversified / Blue Chip / Wesco) into a
single entity.

## Evolution
- [1978] Merged into Berkshire (Dec 30, 1978); raised Blue Chip stake to ~58% and
  brought in Associated Retail Stores.
- [1989] Recalled in the "Mistakes of the First Twenty-Five Years" essay as the vehicle
  through which Buffett bought **Hochschild Kohn**, a Baltimore department store — a
  classic "bargain-purchase folly." Bought at a discount to book with "first-class"
  people and hidden real-estate/LIFO cushions ("How could I miss?"), he was "lucky to
  sell the business for about what I had paid" three years later. The episode taught that
  "good jockeys" can't redeem a "broken-down nag" — see [[Good Business at a Fair Price]],
  [[Mistakes Ledger]].
