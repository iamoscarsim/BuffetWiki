# Johnson & Johnson

The diversified healthcare/consumer-products company, first itemized as a Berkshire equity holding
in [[2006]] (21.3M shares, market $1,409M) — a high-quality, durable-franchise blue chip of the kind
Buffett favors. The position grew into one of Berkshire's larger holdings, but in **2008** Buffett
**sold a portion of J&J** (along with some Procter & Gamble and ConocoPhillips) to raise cash for the
crisis-era opportunities he preferred (the Mars/Wrigley, Goldman Sachs and GE deals) — a reallocation,
not a loss of faith in the businesses.

## Evolution
- [2006] First itemized (~$1.4B); a healthcare blue-chip franchise.
- [2008] Partly **sold** to fund higher-return crisis deployments (a "reluctant" sale of a fine
  business to free up cash).
