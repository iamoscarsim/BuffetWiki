# Bank of New York Mellon

The world's largest custody and securities-services bank, held as a marketable equity first itemized in
the [[2017]] portfolio at **53.3M shares (5.3% of the company), cost $2,230M / market $2,871M**. It is one
of several **bank/financial** positions Berkshire enlarged in 2016–17 (alongside [[Bank of America]], [[US Bancorp]], and [[Wells Fargo]]) — a custody franchise with scale economics and fee-based, capital-light
revenue. Like several mid-sized financial holdings of the era, it bears the fingerprints of **[[Todd Combs]]** and **[[Ted Weschler]]** as well as Buffett.

## Evolution
- [[2017]] New itemized holding: 5.3% of BNY Mellon, ~$2.2B cost.
