# Ben Bridge Jeweler

Ben Bridge Jeweler is a **65-store West Coast jewelry retailer** Berkshire acquired in 2000
in a **half-cash, half-stock** deal — "another purchase we made by phone, prior to any
face-to-face meeting." It is run by fourth-generation owner-managers **Ed and Jon Bridge**
(cousins), of a business started **89 years earlier in Seattle** (1912). The
recommendation came from **[[Barnett Helzberg]]** (from whom Berkshire bought [[Helzberg's Diamond Shops|Helzberg Diamonds]] in 1995), a friend of Ed Bridge.

The business's record is "truly remarkable" — **same-store sales up 9%, 11%, 13%, 10%,
12%, 21% and 7%** over the prior seven years. As with Berkshire's other family-business
acquisitions, the deal turned on a promise of continuity: "It was vital to the family that
the company operate in the future as in the past. No one wanted another jewelry chain to
come in and decimate the organization with ideas about synergy and cost saving" — Buffett
assured Ed and Jon "they would be in charge," and (as with the [[Tatelman Brothers]] and
others) the Bridges shared "a substantial portion of the proceeds" with their co-workers.
