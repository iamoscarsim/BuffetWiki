# BYD

"An amazing Chinese company" in which Berkshire took a **10% interest** in 2008, first mentioned in
the letter's annual-meeting notes as the maker of "a new plug-in electric car" to be displayed on
the exhibition floor ([[2008]]). The investment was sourced and championed by **[[David Sokol]]** and
[[Charlie Munger]] (Munger likened founder Wang Chuanfu to a combination of Edison and Welch),
making it an unusual Berkshire foray into a fast-growing technology/auto-and-battery business in
China — outside Buffett's usual [[Circle of Competence]] but inside his partners'.

## Evolution
- [2008] 10% interest taken; introduced via the meeting exhibit (a BYD plug-in electric car) as
  "an amazing Chinese company."
