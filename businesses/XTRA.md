# XTRA

A leading lessor of **truck trailers** (and other transportation equipment), acquired by
Berkshire in 2001 ([[2001]]) through a tender offer. XTRA came to Buffett's attention via
**Julian Robertson**, who in early 2000 announced he would wind down his **Tiger Fund** and
liquidate all but four large holdings — one of which was XTRA. Buffett called Robertson, was
referred to CEO **Lew Rubin**, and after an initial dead end resumed talks in June 2001 when
Robertson decided to sell his block.

The tender offer was set to expire on **September 11th**, with a standard "out" letting
Berkshire withdraw if the stock market closed before expiration. Rubin endured a wrenching day
— a son-in-law worked in the World Trade Center (he escaped harm) and Berkshire could have
walked — but Berkshire completed the deal. Buffett called trailer leasing "a cyclical business
but one in which we should earn decent returns over time," and signalled an intent to **expand
in leasing**. XTRA was placed in Berkshire's finance and financial-products segment.

## Evolution
- [2001] Acquired by tender offer (Tiger Fund's liquidation the source); a platform for
