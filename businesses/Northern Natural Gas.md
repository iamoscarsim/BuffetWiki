# Northern Natural Gas

A **16,600-mile interstate gas pipeline** running from the Southwest to the Midwest, acquired in
2002 by **[[MidAmerican Energy]]** (MEHC) — one of two pipelines (with **Kern River**) that pushed
Berkshire into the gas-transport business and made MEHC the transporter of ~8% of all U.S. gas
([[2002]]). For Buffett the deal "completes a corporate odyssey of particular interest to Omahans."

Northern Natural began in the 1930s as one of Omaha's premier businesses. Renamed **InterNorth** in
1980, it merged in 1985 with the smaller Houston Natural Gas on the promise of an Omaha headquarters
— promises that "were broken within a year" by Houston Natural's CEO **Ken Lay**, who took the top
job, moved the HQ to Houston, and renamed the company **Enron**. After Enron's 2001 collapse, the
pipeline was pledged to **Dynegy** as loan collateral, passed to Dynegy in a falling-out, and — when
Dynegy hit its own troubles — was sold to MEHC in a quick cash deal (call July 26, contract July
29). "Shortly thereafter Northern Natural returned home." It exemplifies Berkshire as the
fast, certain, cash buyer of choice for distressed sellers.
