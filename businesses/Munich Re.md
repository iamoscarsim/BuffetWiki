# Munich Re

The world's largest reinsurer (Germany), which entered Berkshire's equity portfolio in **2010** as a
**10.5% stake** (cost $2,896M / market $2,924M) — one of two large European reinsurer holdings of the
period alongside the [[Swiss Re]] relationship ([[2010]]). The position fits Buffett's crisis-and-
aftermath tilt toward financially strong insurers trading cheaply, and reflects his comfort with the
reinsurance economics he knows intimately through [[General Re]] and [[Ajit Jain|BH Reinsurance]].

## Evolution
- [2010] New 10.5% common stake disclosed (cost $2,896M / market $2,924M) — Berkshire's largest
  reinsurer equity holding.
- [2011] Position maintained among the largest equity holdings.
