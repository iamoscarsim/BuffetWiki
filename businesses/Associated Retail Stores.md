# Associated Retail Stores

Associated Retail Stores, Inc. was a chain of about 75 popular-priced women's
apparel stores, acquired by Berkshire through the [[Diversified Retailing Company]]
merger in [[1978]]. Launched in Chicago on March 7, 1931 with one store, $3,200,
and partners [[Ben Rosner]] and Leo Simon, it came to Diversified for cash in 1967.

Buffett uses Associated to make a point that recurs throughout the letters: a
business facing "adverse demographic and retailing trends" with no growth can
still be a fine *investment* when run with exceptional skill. [[Ben Rosner]]'s
"merchandising, real estate and cost-containment skills" produced after-tax
returns on capital "often in the 20% area" — high returns despite a stagnant top
line, because little capital was tied up.

## Evolution
- [1978] Acquired via the Diversified merger; ~75 stores; ~20% after-tax returns
  on capital under Ben Rosner despite no growth.
