# Energy Future Holdings

A Texas electric-utility operation (the former TXU, taken private in a 2007 record LBO) whose **bonds**
Berkshire bought to the tune of about **$2 billion** — and which Buffett repeatedly cited as one of
his clearest **mistakes** ([[2011]]). The bet was effectively a wager on natural-gas prices, which
"tanked shortly after our purchase and remained depressed." Berkshire wrote the position down **$1
billion in 2010** and **$390 million in 2011** (carried at $878M), having collected ~$102M/year of
interest along the way. "However things turn out, **I totally miscalculated the gain/loss
probabilities** when I purchased the bonds. In tennis parlance, this was a **major unforced error**
by your chairman" (see [[Mistakes Ledger]]). Notably, this was a Buffett decision made *without*
Munger — and one Buffett owned entirely.

## Evolution
- [2011] ~$2B of EFH bonds confessed as "a big mistake… a major unforced error"; written down $1B
  (2010) + $390M (2011) to $878M as gas prices stayed low.
- [2013] The remaining position essentially wiped out as EFH headed toward bankruptcy — the loss
  realized (~$873M pre-tax), confirming the error.
