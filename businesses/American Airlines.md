# American Airlines

The largest U.S. carrier by traffic (the modern company formed in the 2013 American–US Airways merger —
folding in the very **[[USAir]]** that gave Buffett his "gold medal" mistake), one of the four major
carriers Berkshire bought in [[2016]] as part of its airline re-entry ([[Evolving Views — Airlines]]). In
the [[2016]] letter the position sits within the "Others" line of the portfolio table rather than among
the fifteen itemized holdings, but Buffett confirms Berkshire's "large investments in **America's four
major carriers**." There is a certain irony in Berkshire ending up an owner of the successor to USAir.

## Evolution
- [[2016]] New holding (in "Others"): part of the all-four-carriers basket bet. The modern American
  Airlines contains the former US Airways/USAir.
- [[2020]] **Sold in full** during the COVID-19 travel collapse (announced May 2020), at a loss — gone
  from the year-end portfolio. See [[Evolving Views — Airlines]].
