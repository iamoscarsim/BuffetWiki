# General Motors

The largest U.S. automaker, held as a marketable equity first itemized in the [[2017]] portfolio at
**44.5M shares (3.2% of the company), cost $1,343M / market $1,825M**. A value-priced, cash-generative
position of the kind associated with **[[Todd Combs]]** and **[[Ted Weschler]]** (the stake was initiated
several years earlier and grew). GM sits oddly beside Berkshire's old aversion to capital-intensive,
commodity-cyclical manufacturers — bought on a low earnings multiple rather than as a classic franchise.

## Evolution
- [[2017]] Itemized at 3.2% of GM, ~$1.3B cost (a value/cyclical position).
