# Delta Air Lines

One of the four major U.S. carriers, bought by Berkshire in [[2016]] as part of its surprise re-entry into
the airline industry ([[Evolving Views — Airlines]]). Disclosed in the [[2016]] portfolio at **54.9M
shares (7.5% of Delta), cost $2,299M / market $2,702M**. The stake reflected the thesis that a
consolidated, capacity-disciplined U.S. industry had finally become investable — a sharp break from the
[[USAir]] aversion. Of the four carriers, Delta would become Berkshire's largest airline position.

## Evolution
- [[2016]] New holding: 7.5% of Delta, ~$2.3B cost. Part of the all-four-carriers basket bet.
- [[2019]] Stake grew to ~11% (the largest of Berkshire's four carriers; ~$4.1B market value).
- [[2020]] **Sold in full** during the COVID-19 travel collapse (announced May 2020), at a loss — gone
  from the year-end portfolio. See [[Evolving Views — Airlines]].
