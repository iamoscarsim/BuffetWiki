# Business Wire

The leading distributor of corporate press releases and regulatory disclosures (operating in 150
countries for 25,000 clients), acquired by Berkshire in 2005 ([[2005]]). The deal was sourced by a
letter: after a November 12, 2005 *Wall Street Journal* article on Berkshire's unusual acquisition
practices, president **[[Cathy Baron Tamraz]]** wrote to Buffett — "I believe [we] fit the profile
of Berkshire Hathaway subsidiary companies" — and won him over with a line he loved: "We run a
**tight ship** and keep unnecessary spending under wraps. No secretaries or management layers here.
Yet we'll invest big dollars to gain a technological advantage." Berkshire reached agreement with
founder **Lorry Lokey** (who started the company in 1961 and had just made Tamraz CEO).

## Evolution
- [2005] Acquired after Cathy Baron Tamraz's letter; founder Lorry Lokey; a lean, tech-forward
  press-release distributor.
