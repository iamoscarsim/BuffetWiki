# CORT Business Services

CORT Business Services is the U.S. leader in **"rent-to-rent" furniture** — furnishings
leased mainly to offices and to temporary apartment occupants — acquired by Berkshire in
2000 for about **$386 million in cash** and placed with the 80%-owned subsidiary **[[Wesco Financial|Wesco]]**. Buffett is careful to distinguish it from "rent-to-own" operations
(which sell home furnishings and electronics to buyers with limited income and poor
credit); CORT operated **117 showrooms** and is run by CEO **Paul Arnold**.

The deal is a classic Berkshire serendipity tale: on November 23, 1999, Buffett received a
one-page fax from **Bruce Cort** (an airplane broker — no relation to the company — who had
sold Berkshire a jet in 1986 and not been in touch for a decade) appending a *Washington
Post* article about an **aborted buyout** of CORT. Buffett "immediately printed out its SEC
filings and liked what I saw," met Arnold on November 29, and found "the right ingredients:
a fine though unglamorous business, an outstanding manager, and a price (going by that on
the failed deal) that made sense."
