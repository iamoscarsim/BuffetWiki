# Swiss Re

The Swiss reinsurance giant. Berkshire took a **3.2% common-stock stake** (cost $773M / market $530M
at year-end) that first appears in the 2008 portfolio table ([[2008]]). The relationship deepened
during the crisis: Berkshire became both an equity holder and a provider of capital and reinsurance
support to Swiss Re — the kind of deal only a balance sheet of Berkshire's [[Financial Strength|strength]]
could offer a stressed reinsurer, echoing the logic of the [[General Re]] thesis (volatility-tolerant
capital as the durable edge in reinsurance).
