# Garan

A manufacturer of **children's apparel**, best known for the **Garanimals** mix-and-match line,
acquired by Berkshire in 2002 ([[2002]]) alongside **[[CTB]]** as one of the year's two
sub-threshold deals (together >$60M pre-tax). Apparel is a "tough" business, but Garan earns
"decent returns on capital" on the strength of its brand and management — **Seymour Lichtenstein**,
"responsible for [its] impressive record," stayed on. Garan joined Berkshire's apparel group
alongside **[[Fruit of the Loom]]**.
