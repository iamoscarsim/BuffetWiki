# Paramount Global

The U.S. media conglomerate (CBS, Paramount Pictures, the Paramount+ streaming service), a **new 2022
equity position** in which the [[2022]] letter lists Berkshire as **the largest owner** (one of the "eight
giants" Berkshire topped). It was an uncharacteristic foray into a streaming-pressured media business
([[Evolving Views — Newspapers|media economics]] having long worried Buffett) — and it ended badly:
Berkshire **sold the entire stake at a loss in 2023–24**, with Buffett later taking personal blame ("it
was 100% my decision… we lost quite a bit of money").

## Evolution
- [[2022]] New holding; Berkshire its largest shareholder. *(Sold in full at a loss by 2024 — a Buffett-
  owned mistake.)*
