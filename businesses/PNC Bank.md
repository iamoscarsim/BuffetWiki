# PNC Bank

PNC Bank Corporation, the Pittsburgh-based bank, was a new Berkshire common-stock holding in
1994 — 19,453,300 shares (~8.3%; cost $503.0M / market $411.0M, one of the few positions
under water at year-end), contributing $10M of look-through earnings ([[1994]]). It joins
[[Wells Fargo]] (and, briefly, [[PNC Bank|PNC]]) among Buffett's bank holdings of the period —
a continuation of his "buy well-managed banks at fair prices" approach. PNC appears only
briefly in the letters before fading from the disclosed list.
