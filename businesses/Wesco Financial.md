# Wesco Financial

Wesco Financial Corporation was an 80%-owned subsidiary of [[Blue Chip Stamps]],
managed in [[1977]] by Louis Vincenti. Originally a California savings and loan
holding company, Wesco became a diversified vehicle within the Berkshire/Blue
Chip orbit, later holding insurance operations and equity investments, and for
years served as a smaller, separately reported mirror of Berkshire's approach.
[[Charlie Munger]] chaired Wesco for decades. Berkshire eventually acquired the
remaining minority and absorbed Wesco fully.

## Evolution
- [1977] 80%-owned subsidiary of Blue Chip Stamps, run by Louis Vincenti; "made
  good progress."
- [1983] After the Blue Chip merger, Berkshire's net Wesco interest rose to 80%.
  **[[Charlie Munger]] became Wesco's Chairman** when Louie Vincenti retired at 77 due
  to genuine ill health. Holds Mutual Savings and Loan and Precision Steel.
- [1996] **Expanded its insurance operations** by taking on **[[Kansas Bankers Surety]]** — the
  $75M bank-insurance specialty acquisition was placed with Wesco, "which has wanted to expand
  its insurance operations," because of KBS's relatively small size.
- [2000] **[[CORT Business Services]]** (~$386M cash; the national rent-to-rent furniture leader)
  was purchased *through Wesco*, deepening Wesco's role as a vehicle for Berkshire's smaller
  acquisitions. Details appear in Wesco's own annual reports.
