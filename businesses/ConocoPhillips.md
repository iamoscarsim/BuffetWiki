# ConocoPhillips

The U.S. integrated oil-and-gas company, first itemized as a Berkshire equity holding in [[2006]]
(17.9M shares, ~1.1%, market $1,291M). The position became one of Buffett's most-cited **mistakes of
commission**: in **2008**, "**with oil and gas prices near their peak**," he greatly enlarged the
stake (to ~$7B), only to watch energy prices collapse — a poorly-timed purchase he confessed cost
Berkshire "several billion dollars" and described candidly in the [[2008]] letter. Berkshire pared
the holding over the following years.

## Evolution
- [2006] First itemized (~1.1%, $1.3B market) — an energy holding.
- [2008] **Buffett dramatically increases the stake near peak oil prices** — "without urging from
  Charlie or anyone else, I bought a large amount… when oil and gas prices were near their peak"; the
  subsequent price crash made it a multi-billion-dollar timing error (see [[Mistakes Ledger]]).
- [2009] Position trimmed; Buffett reiterates the buy was "a major unforced error."
