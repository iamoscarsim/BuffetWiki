# Wal-Mart

The world's largest retailer, a Berkshire equity position established in 2005 ([[2005]], ~19.9M
shares, 0.5%, cost $944M / market $933M) — and a company Buffett admired enough to have repeatedly
voted it Fortune's "Most Admired." Berkshire's relationship with Wal-Mart predates the stock: in 2003
it had bought Wal-Mart's **[[McLane]]** distribution subsidiary in a celebrated handshake deal.
Buffett has called passing up a *larger* Wal-Mart stake years earlier (over a fraction of a dollar in
price) one of his costly errors of omission. The position was modestly sized and later sold.

## Evolution
- [2005] New position (0.5%, ~$933M); the company whose McLane unit Berkshire had bought in 2003.
