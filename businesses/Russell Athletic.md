# Russell Athletic

A leading producer of **athletic apparel and uniforms** (Russell Corp.), acquired in 2006 ([[2006]])
as a **tuck-in under [[Fruit of the Loom]]** for about **$1.2 billion** (including assumed debt) —
the largest of the year's bolt-on deals. Together with Fruit's near-simultaneous purchase of **VF
Corp.'s intimate-apparel business**, it added about **$2.2 billion of sales and 23,000 employees**
to Fruit. The logic is Buffett's "leverage the managerial talent already with us": both deals were
placed under **John Holland**, Fruit's CEO, "who [has] already shown [his] stuff at Berkshire."
