# Charter Communications

U.S. cable and broadband operator (the "Spectrum" brand), held as a marketable equity disclosed in the
[[2016]] portfolio at **6.8M shares (2.5% of Charter), cost $1,210M / market $1,955M**. The position is
characteristic of the holdings run by **[[Todd Combs]]** and **[[Ted Weschler]]** rather than a classic
Buffett franchise pick — a leveraged, consolidating cable business bought during the industry's
broadband-driven re-rating.

## Evolution
- [[2016]] New holding: 2.5% of Charter, ~$1.2B cost (a Combs/Weschler-style position).
