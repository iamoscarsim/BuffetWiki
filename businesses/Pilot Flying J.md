# Pilot Flying J

The largest **travel-center / truck-stop operator** in North America, and Berkshire's one "sensible
stand-alone purchase" of [[2017]] — a year in which sky-high prices kept it out of nearly every other
deal it reviewed. Berkshire bought a **38.6% partnership interest** in PFJ, a Knoxville company with
about **$20 billion of annual volume**, ~**750 locations**, and 27,000 associates (gasoline, diesel,
food, and — Buffett notes — **5,200 showers**).

PFJ "has been run from the get-go by the remarkable **Haslam family**": founder "Big Jim" Haslam started
"with a dream and a gas station 60 years ago," and son **Jimmy Haslam** now runs it. A **contractual
agreement steps Berkshire's interest to 80% in 2023**, with the Haslams retaining 20%. The deal had a
personal-network genesis characteristic of Berkshire's [[Acquisition Criteria|sourcing]]: the Clayton and
Haslam families are long-time Knoxville friends, and **[[Kevin Clayton]]**'s praise of a Berkshire
affiliation (and of the Haslams to Buffett) "helped cement the PFJ deal."

## Evolution
- [[2017]] Bought a **38.6%** partnership interest in PFJ (the Haslam family's travel-center leader,
  ~$20B volume); contracted to reach **80% in 2023**. Berkshire's only stand-alone acquisition of the year.
- [[2020]] **A Knoxville jewel (now "Pilot Travel Centers").** Featured in the "Tale of Two Cities" essay
  alongside **[[Clayton Homes]]**: each Knoxville company was "started by a young man who… stayed put in
  Knoxville" with little capital ("Big Jim" Haslam bought a service station for **$6,000** in 1958), and
  each now earns **>$1 billion pre-tax**; together they employ ~47,000. Berkshire's stake remains **38%,
  "headed for 80% in 2023."** Buffett credits **[[Kevin Clayton]]**'s endorsement of a Berkshire
  affiliation for cementing the deal — "satisfied customers are a store's best salespeople."
- [[2021]] **Headed to control.** The 38.6% stake (equity-method) covered a travel-center leader with
  **$45 billion of revenue**; Buffett confirms Berkshire will buy an additional interest **"early in 2023"**
  to reach **80%** and fully consolidate Pilot's earnings, assets and liabilities.
- [[2022]] Reconfirmed as a jointly-controlled holding (38.6%, equity-method); Berkshire's contracted move
  to **80% closed in January 2023**, after which Pilot's earnings, assets and liabilities consolidate.
