# Chevron

Integrated oil major, a **new position** in the [[2020]] portfolio: **48.5M shares (2.5% of Chevron),
cost $4,024M / market $4,096M**. Bought in Q4 2020 — alongside the **[[Occidental Petroleum]]** preferred
— it marked a renewed Berkshire appetite for large, well-capitalized **energy** producers at a time of
depressed oil prices, valued for their cash generation and dividends. Unlike many 2020 dabblings, Chevron
would **grow into one of Berkshire's largest equity holdings** over the following years.

## Evolution
- [[2020]] New holding: 2.5% of Chevron, ~$4.0B cost — part of a 2020 energy tilt (with Occidental).
  *(Later built into a top-tier position.)*
- [[2021]] Itemized at **2.0% of Chevron** (38.2M shares, cost $3,420M / market $4,488M) — the energy
  position growing as Buffett leaned into oil majors; it would be enlarged dramatically in 2022.
- [[2022]] **Built into a top holding — Berkshire its largest owner.** Buffett dramatically enlarged the
  Chevron stake during 2022's energy run-up; the letter lists Berkshire as **the largest owner of Chevron**
  (one of "eight giants" of the S&P 500 Berkshire topped, alongside [[Occidental Petroleum|Occidental]]).
  It became one of Berkshire's four or five largest equity positions — a major, deliberate bet on a
  well-capitalized integrated oil major.
