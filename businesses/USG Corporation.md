# USG Corporation

The leading U.S. maker of gypsum wallboard (Sheetrock), a Berkshire equity holding first itemized in
[[2006]] (**19.0%** of the company, market $936M) — a building-products bet tied to the asbestos and
housing themes of the era (USG had emerged from an asbestos-driven bankruptcy reorganization).
Berkshire's stake gave it ~19% of a cyclical, housing-exposed business; during the financial crisis
Berkshire **backstopped a USG rights offering with convertible notes** (2008), and the holding
persisted until USG was acquired by Germany's Knauf in 2019, when Berkshire's shares were cashed out.
