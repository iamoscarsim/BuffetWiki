# Gannett

Gannett Co., the newspaper and media group (publisher of *USA Today*), was a new Berkshire
common-stock holding in 1994 — 6,854,500 shares (~4.9%; cost $335.2M / market $365.0M),
contributing $4M of look-through earnings ([[1994]]). It is a modest position taken even as
Buffett's broader view of newspaper economics had turned cautious (see [[Evolving Views — Newspapers]]). Gannett appears only briefly in the letters and quietly exits the disclosed
holdings in later years.
