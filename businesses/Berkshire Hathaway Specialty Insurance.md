# Berkshire Hathaway Specialty Insurance

**BHSI**, the **commercial-insurance** operation **[[Ajit Jain]] formed in June 2013** — Berkshire's
deliberate entry into large-company property/casualty, excess and specialty lines ([[2013]]). It was
"instantly accepted by both major insurance brokers and corporate risk managers throughout America,"
because no other insurer can match Berkshire's [[Financial Strength|financial strength]] — the
guarantee that "legitimate claims arising many years in the future will be paid promptly and fully"
(the same promise-keeping edge that drives the [[General Re]] and [[Equitas]] businesses).

BHSI is led by **Peter Eastwood**, "an experienced underwriter… widely respected in the insurance
world," who assembled a team writing business with "many Fortune 500 companies." Buffett predicted it
"will be a major asset for Berkshire… generat[ing] volume in the billions within a few years," and it
grew rapidly into a global commercial insurer.

## Evolution
- [2013] Launched in June under Peter Eastwood; Berkshire's entry into commercial insurance, riding
  its unmatched balance-sheet strength; expected to reach billions in premium volume.
