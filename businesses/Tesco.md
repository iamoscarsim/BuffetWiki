# Tesco

The British supermarket giant, first itemized as a Berkshire equity holding in [[2006]] (229.7M
shares, ~2.9%, market $1,820M) — bought partly as a way to gain **foreign-currency exposure through
foreign equities** as Berkshire wound down its direct currency bet (see [[Trade Deficit and the Dollar]]). The holding ended badly and became a candid **mistake**: as Tesco's business deteriorated
(culminating in a 2014 accounting scandal), Buffett was slow to sell, and in the [[2014]] letter he
confessed that "**dawdling**" cost Berkshire an after-tax loss of **$444 million** — "an attentive
investor… would have sold Tesco shares earlier. I made a big mistake with this investment by being
slow to wake up."
