# Benjamin Moore

Benjamin Moore is the **117-year-old paint maker** Berkshire acquired in 2000 for **$1
billion in cash**. A director of the company, **Bob Mundheim** — whom Buffett knew and
"held in very high regard" from his time as [[Salomon]]'s general counsel "during some
difficult times" (the 1991 scandal) — called in July to ask if Berkshire might be
interested. Buffett and Munger met past and present CEOs **Richard Roob and Yvan Dupuy** in
late August, "liked them … liked the business; and … made a $1 billion cash offer **on the
spot**"; the board approved in October and the deal closed in December.

Benjamin Moore's distribution moat is its network of **thousands of independent dealers**,
"a vital asset to its business." It is the "paint" leg of Berkshire's 2000 expansion into
"such cutting-edge industries as brick, carpet, insulation and paint." Buffett's pitch:
"Make sure you specify our product for your next paint job."
