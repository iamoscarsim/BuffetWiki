# Anheuser-Busch

The American brewing giant (Budweiser), a new Berkshire equity position established in 2005 ([[2005]],
43,854,200 shares, ~5.6%, cost $2,133M / market $1,884M) — an understandable consumer-brand franchise
of the kind Buffett favors. The holding proved short-lived: Berkshire sold most of it in 2008 when
**InBev acquired Anheuser-Busch**, ending the position with a modest result. Buffett later remarked
he had mis-timed trims during the takeover episode.

## Evolution
- [2005] New position (~5.6%, $1.9B market); a branded-beverage franchise.
- [2008] Largely sold into the **InBev** takeover of Anheuser-Busch.
