# James Hambrick

CEO of **[[Lubrizol]]** since 2004 and the manager whose record drew Berkshire to the specialty-
chemicals maker it bought in 2011 ([[2011]]). Under Hambrick, Lubrizol's pre-tax profit grew from
**$147 million to $1,085 million**. Buffett called him "a **disciplined buyer and a superb
operator**" and was "eager to expand his managerial domain" via bolt-on acquisitions in the
fragmented specialty-chemical field.

## Evolution
- [2011] Introduced as Lubrizol's CEO at the acquisition; pre-tax profit grew 7× under his
  leadership; tasked with continued bolt-on growth.
