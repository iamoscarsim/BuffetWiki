# Jack Ringwalt

Jack Ringwalt was the founder of [[National Indemnity Company]], which Buffett
bought in 1967. In the [[1978]] letter Buffett credits NICO's continued
underwriting excellence not only to current managers like [[Phil Liesche]] but
"equally upon the business talents of Jack Ringwalt, founder of National
Indemnity, whose operating philosophy remains etched upon the company." Ringwalt's
willingness to walk away from underpriced business is the cultural source of
Berkshire's [[Insurance Underwriting Discipline]]. (He is the brother of John
Ringwalt, who ran the homestate insurance operation.)
