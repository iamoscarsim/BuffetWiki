# David Dodd

David L. Dodd was Buffett's teacher and friend for 38 years, a lifelong Columbia
University professor, and co-author with [[Ben Graham]] of *Security Analysis*. Buffett
devotes a warm eulogy to him in the [[1988]] letter, on his death at age 93 — "any
long-time shareholder of Berkshire is appreciably wealthier because of the indirect
influence he had upon our company."

Dodd "personally encouraged and educated" Buffett from the moment he arrived at
Columbia and continued the education "through dozens of letters… right up until his
death." Buffett ranks him with Graham as the only finance professors he ever saw who
truly matched the subject, and offers as proof "the record of his students: no other
teacher of investments has sent forth so many who have achieved unusual success." Dodd
"could practice as well as preach," and Buffett notes his investing results outshone
even Keynes's — because, unlike Keynes (a reformed market-timer), "Dave was right from
the start." "Our prosperity is the fruit of their intellectual tree."
