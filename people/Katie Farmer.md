# Katie Farmer

Became **CEO of [[Burlington Northern Santa Fe|BNSF]] at the end of 2020**, succeeding the retiring
**[[Carl Ice]]** (she had been his "number two"). In a COVID-hit year, Ice and Farmer "did an
extraordinary job in controlling expenses while navigating a significant downturn," *increasing* BNSF's
profit margin by **2.9 percentage points despite a 7% decline in volume** — prompting Buffett's verdict:
"**Your railroad is in good hands**" ([[2020]]). A long-time BNSF executive, Farmer's elevation continued
Berkshire's pattern of promoting proven insiders to run its giants.
