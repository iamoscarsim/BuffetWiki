# Jack Bogle

Founder of The Vanguard Group and creator of the first retail **index fund**, whom Buffett honors in the
[[2016]] letter as the person who has "**done the most for American investors**" — "If a statue is ever
erected to honor [that person], the hands-down choice should be Jack Bogle." For decades Bogle urged
investors into **ultra-low-cost index funds**, "frequently mocked" early on by an industry that profited
from the opposite advice, and amassed "only a tiny percentage" of the wealth that flowed to managers who
"promised… large rewards while delivering them nothing." Buffett's tribute caps his **[[Index Funds and Fees|"Bet"]]** essay; the contender fund Buffett named in the wager was a low-cost Vanguard S&P 500 fund —
Bogle's creation. "He is a hero to them and to me."
