# Ike Friedman

Ike Friedman is the head of the Friedman family that runs **[[Borsheim's]]**, the Omaha
jewelry store Berkshire bought 80% of in early 1989. He is part of [[Rose Blumkin]]'s
extended family — his mother Rebecca was Mrs. B's sister — and brings to jewelry "the
same approach that the Blumkins bring to the furniture business."

Introduced in the [[1988]] letter, Ike joined his father Louis in the business in 1950,
and the next generations followed (his son Alan; sons-in-law Marvin Cohn and Donald
Yale). Buffett's trust in him is the deal's "ultimate testimonial": Borsheim's had no
audited financials, yet Berkshire "didn't take inventory, verify receivables or audit
the operation in any way — Ike simply told us what was so." "I can assure you that
those who put their trust in Ike Friedman and his family will never be disappointed."

## Evolution
