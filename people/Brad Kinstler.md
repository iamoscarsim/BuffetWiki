# Brad Kinstler

Brad Kinstler first appears in the [[1993]] letter as the head of Berkshire's **workers'
compensation** insurance business, one of four insurance units (with the homestate operation
under Rod Eldred, the Kizer family's credit-card operation, and [[National Indemnity Company|National Indemnity]]'s auto/general-liability book under Don Wurster) that "achieved
excellent results," together producing "a significant underwriting profit and substantial
float." A trusted Berkshire operating manager, Kinstler later runs [[Fechheimer Brothers]]
and then [[See's Candies]] — a relationship later letters will track.

## Evolution
- [1993] Introduced as head of Berkshire's workers' compensation insurance business.
- [2000] **Moved from insurance (Cypress) to run [[Fechheimer Brothers]]**, Berkshire's Cincinnati
  uniform maker — a rare cross-business transfer of a Berkshire manager. "We seldom move Berkshire
  managers from one enterprise to another, but maybe we should try it more often: **Brad is hitting
  home runs in his new job, just as he always did at Cypress**." (Michael Stearns took over Cypress.)
  *(Kinstler would later move again, to run [[See's Candies]].)*
- [2005] **Takes over [[See's Candies]]** as **[[Chuck Huggins]]** retires after 34 years — his
  third Berkshire posting (after Cypress Insurance and Fechheimer). "Brad's record made him an
  obvious choice," a rare instance of Berkshire shifting a manager between businesses.
