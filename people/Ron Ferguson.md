# Ron Ferguson

Ron Ferguson is the longtime CEO of **[[General Re]]**, the reinsurance giant Berkshire
acquired in 1998. Buffett introduces him as the leader under whom General Re's reputation
"for quality, integrity and professionalism in reinsurance … has been burnished still
more," adding that "Berkshire can add absolutely nothing to the skills of General Re's and
Cologne Re's managers. On the contrary, there is a lot that they can teach us." He and
Buffett had known each other for years, each company having reinsured business with the
other.

Buffett's promise to Ferguson was **freedom**: to "exercise the discipline of the past
while increasing the proportion of its business that is retained, expanding its product
line, and widening its geographical coverage" — unfettered by the quarterly-earnings-
smoothness pressure that constrains public reinsurers (see [[General Re]], [[Financial Strength]]). *(General Re's subsequent underwriting and reserve problems would complicate
this optimism — a thread later letters take up.)*

## Evolution
- [1998] Introduced as General Re's CEO at the $22B acquisition; praised for the company's
  reputation; given a mandate to retain more business and expand internationally on the
  strength of Berkshire's balance sheet.
