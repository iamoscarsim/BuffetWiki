# Louie Blumkin

Louie Blumkin, son of [[Rose Blumkin]], was president of the [[Nebraska Furniture Mart]]
for many years and, in the [[1983]] letter, "widely regarded as the shrewdest buyer
of furniture and appliances in the country." Buffett praises the whole family —
Louie and his three sons — for "the Blumkin business ability, work ethic, and, most
important, character." Louie said he had the best teacher (Mrs. B) and Mrs. B said
she had the best student; "they're both right." Louie became the operating bridge to
the next generation of family management at NFM.

## Evolution
- [1983] President of NFM at the time of Berkshire's purchase; praised as the
  country's shrewdest home-furnishings buyer.
