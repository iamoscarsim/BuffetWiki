# Matt Rose

CEO of **[[Burlington Northern Santa Fe]]** at the time of Berkshire's 2009–2010 acquisition of the
railroad — "whom we trust and admire" ([[2009]]). Buffett cited Rose's leadership as part of the
rationale for the deal, which Berkshire was willing to fund despite paying ~30% of the price in
its own (in Buffett's view undervalued) stock. Rose ran BNSF as the centerpiece of Berkshire's new,
deliberately capital-intensive **"regulated utility and railroad"** reporting sector — a business
built on a "**social compact**" of essential service in exchange for fair regulated returns, and one
expected to absorb "tens of billions" of incremental equity capital over time.

## Evolution
- [2009] Named as BNSF's trusted CEO in the acquisition announcement.
- [2010] Continues to run BNSF as a wholly-owned Berkshire subsidiary after the February close.
