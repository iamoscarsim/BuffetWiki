# Jorge Paulo Lemann

"A renowned Brazilian businessman and philanthropist," co-founder of **3G Capital**, and Buffett's
partner in Berkshire's food-industry investments ([[2012]]). A "**long-time friend of mine and an
extraordinary manager**," Lemann led the investor group that joined Berkshire to buy **[[H.J. Heinz]]** (2013) and later to engineer the **[[Kraft Heinz]]** merger (2015).

3G's hallmark is intense operational discipline — zero-based budgeting and aggressive cost reduction
— a style that complements (and contrasts with) Berkshire's famously hands-off [[Decentralization| decentralization]]. The recurring division of labor: Berkshire brings capital and permanence; 3G
brings management. "We couldn't be in better company."

## Evolution
- [2012] Introduced as Berkshire's 50/50 partner in the Heinz holding company (deal announced Feb 2013).
- [2013]–[2015] Partnership deepens — Heinz closes, then merges with Kraft into [[Kraft Heinz]] (2015),
  with Lemann's 3G running operations.
