# Mike Goldberg

Mike Goldberg took over parent-company responsibility for most of Berkshire's
insurance operations in 1982, relieving Buffett of that role ([[1982]]): "Planning,
recruitment, and monitoring all have shown significant improvement since Mike
replaced me." He becomes a key figure in building Berkshire's insurance group
through the lean mid-1980s and is associated with recruiting the next generation of
insurance talent — most consequentially [[Ajit Jain]], who would build the
reinsurance operation into one of Berkshire's crown jewels.

## Evolution
- [1982] Assumed leadership of Berkshire's insurance operations from Buffett.
