# Bill Child

Bill Child is the CEO of [[R.C. Willey Home Furnishings]], the Utah home-furnishings chain
Berkshire acquired in mid-1995 ([[1995]]). He **took over the business from his father-in-law
in 1954**, when sales were about **$250,000**, and — "aided by his brother, Sheldon" — built
it to **$257M of sales** and over half of Utah's furniture market, embodying Mae West's
"it's not what you've got, it's what you do with what you've got." When Child, for estate-tax
and diversification reasons, signaled interest in selling (after Irv Blumkin of [[Nebraska Furniture Mart]] had told him of the Blumkins' happy Berkshire relationship), the deal came
together with unusual ease — figures, a value letter, "and found our personal chemistry to be
perfect." Child runs R.C. Willey autonomously, the kind of owner-manager Buffett prizes: "We
are lucky to have Bill and Sheldon associated with us."

## Evolution
- [1995] Introduced as R.C. Willey's CEO (since 1954); built it from $250K to $257M of sales;
  sold to Berkshire and stayed on to run it autonomously.
- [1997] Paid the referral forward: asked for industry standouts, Child named **[[Star Furniture]]**
  of Houston (from the Blumkins' original list), leading to Berkshire's purchase — "Bill is the
  perfect partner."
- [1999] **"A managerial story you will never read elsewhere."** A devout Mormon, Child keeps
  [[R.C. Willey Home Furnishings|R.C. Willey]] **closed on Sundays** even though it's the favorite
  shopping day — yet grew sales from $250K (1954) to **$342M (1999)**. Expanding to Boise against
  seven-day rivals, he **personally bought the land and built the ~$9M store**, offering to sell it
  to Berkshire at cost only if it succeeded (and to eat the loss himself if it failed — "If there
  was to be failure because of his religious beliefs, he wanted to take the blow personally"). It
  succeeded; he turned it over and **refused even interest** on the two years of tied-up capital.
  "You can understand why the opportunity to partner with people like Bill Child causes me to **tap
  dance to work every morning**."
