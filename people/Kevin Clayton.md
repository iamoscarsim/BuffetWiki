# Kevin Clayton

CEO of **[[Clayton Homes]]** at its 2003 sale to Berkshire and the son of founder **[[Jim Clayton]]**
([[2003]]). When Buffett, prompted by Jim's autobiography, called to express his admiration, "it
became clear that he was both **able and a straight-shooter**" — and that judgment, with the public
financials and Buffett's hard-won knowledge of the industry (the Oakwood debt loss), was enough to
make an offer. Kevin stayed on to run Clayton, which grew its lending and acquired bankrupt rival
Oakwood's assets, and proved a model of disciplined manufactured-housing finance through the
2008–09 housing crisis.
