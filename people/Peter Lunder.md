# Peter Lunder

Peter Lunder is the nephew of **[[Harold Alfond]]** and his partner in building
[[Dexter Shoe]]. He joined the company in 1958, two years after Alfond founded it, and the
two together grew it into a 7.5-million-pair-a-year shoemaker with 77 retail outlets and a
leading share of U.S. golf-shoe production ([[1993]]). Like Alfond, Lunder took **Berkshire
stock** rather than cash in the November 1993 merger and continued to run the business he
"dearly love[s]" — Buffett's preferred arrangement with owner-managers. (See [[Dexter Shoe]]
for the later reassessment of the stock-funded deal.)
