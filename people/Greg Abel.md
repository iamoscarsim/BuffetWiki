# Greg Abel

First named in the [[2002]] letter as **[[David Sokol]]'s "key associate"** at MidAmerican Energy
Holdings — "**Dave Sokol… and Greg Abel… are huge assets for Berkshire. They are dealmakers, and
they are managers.**" Abel, a Canadian-born accountant by training, ran the operating side of
**[[MidAmerican Energy]]** (later Berkshire Hathaway Energy) and built it, through Sokol's and then
his own leadership, into one of Berkshire's largest divisions — a sprawling regulated-utility and
pipeline empire.

His importance to the wiki grows far beyond this debut: after Sokol's 2011 departure Abel became
chairman/CEO of Berkshire Hathaway Energy, was made a **Berkshire vice chairman (non-insurance
operations) in 2018**, and in 2021 was publicly **designated Buffett's successor as Berkshire CEO**
— the eventual answer to the succession question the letters circle for decades.

## Evolution
- [2002] First mention: Sokol's "key associate" at MEHC; "dealmakers… and managers… huge assets
  for Berkshire." (Later: built Berkshire Hathaway Energy, became vice chairman in 2018, and the
  designated successor CEO in 2021.)
- [2011]–[2013] **Sole leader of [[MidAmerican Energy|MidAmerican / Berkshire Hathaway Energy]]** after
  [[David Sokol]]'s 2011 departure; drives the renewables build-out and the NV Energy/AltaLink
  acquisitions; one of the "powerhouse five" CEOs.
- [2014] **Named "world-leading" by Munger.** In the Golden Anniversary essay (see [[The Berkshire System]]), Munger calls Abel and **[[Ajit Jain]]** "proven performers who would probably be
  under-described as 'world-class'… **In some important ways, each is a better business executive than
  Buffett**" — and predicts neither would leave nor want to change the Berkshire system. The strongest
  public signal yet of Abel's succession trajectory.
- [[2017]] **Named Berkshire Vice Chairman (non-insurance).** "I've saved the best for last": early in
  2018 the board elected Abel (and **[[Ajit Jain]]**) a **director and Vice Chairman** — Abel "**oversees
  the rest of our businesses**" (everything outside insurance), while Buffett and Munger "focus on
  investments and capital allocation." "Berkshire's blood flows through [his] veins… The character of
  each man matches his talents." The formal beginning of the operating handoff (and a long step toward
  his 2021 designation as successor CEO). See [[The Berkshire System]].
- [[2018]] **"Really good news."** A year into the new structure, Buffett pronounces it a clear success:
  with Abel over all non-insurance operations and **[[Ajit Jain]]** over insurance, "**Berkshire is now
  far better managed than when I alone was supervising operations. Ajit and Greg have rare talents, and
  Berkshire blood flows through their veins.**" "These moves were overdue."
- [[2021]] **The operating heir, ever more visible.** Abel's signed **BHE report** accompanies the letter
  (renewables/transmission), and he attended **[[Paul Andrews Jr|Paul Andrews]]'** memorial with Buffett.
  BHE — which he built with David Sokol from nothing into "a utility powerhouse" — earned a record $4B.
  *(Abel had been publicly confirmed as Buffett's successor-CEO in 2021.)*
- [[2023]] **"Ready to be CEO of Berkshire tomorrow."** In the "What is it with Omaha?" section Buffett
  states it plainly: Abel "runs all non-insurance operations for Berkshire — **and in all respects is ready
  to be CEO of Berkshire tomorrow**." (Canadian-born, he had lived in Omaha blocks from Buffett in the
  1990s without their meeting.) Abel also accompanied Buffett to **Tokyo** to meet the managements of the
  five **[[Japanese Trading Houses]]** before Berkshire raised those stakes to ~9% — a sign of his widening
  capital-allocation role. The clearest succession statement yet.
- [[2024]] **The handoff named.** Buffett states it outright: "**At 94, it won't be long before Greg Abel
  replaces me as CEO and will be writing the annual letters.**" Abel "shares the Berkshire creed that a
  'report' is what a CEO annually owes to owners," and "has vividly shown his ability to act" when
  opportunities appear "as did Charlie." He is expected to hold the **[[Japanese Trading Houses]]** position
  "for many decades." *(Abel formally became CEO at the start of 2025.)*
