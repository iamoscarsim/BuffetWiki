# Eitan Wertheimer

Chairman of **[[ISCAR]]**, the Israeli cutting-tools company, and the man whose **1¼-page letter**
(October 25, 2005) prompted Berkshire's **first-ever acquisition of a non-U.S. business** ([[2006]]).
Buffett "then knew nothing" of Wertheimer, but the letter — "I am writing to introduce you to
ISCAR… Our conclusion is that Berkshire Hathaway would be the ideal home for ISCAR" — "made the
quality of the company and the character of its management leap off the page." After meetings in
Omaha and a Buffett/Munger visit to Israel, Berkshire bought 80% for $4B; the Wertheimer family kept
20% as "our valued partner." Buffett called Eitan, CEO Jacob Harpaz and their team "**true managerial
magicians**."
