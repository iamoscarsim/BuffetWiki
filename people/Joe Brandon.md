# Joe Brandon

Appointed **CEO of [[General Re]]** in September 2001, charged — with new president **Tad
Montross** — with restoring the underwriting discipline that had collapsed catastrophically by
[[2001]] (the $800M of belated prior-year [[Loss Reserving|loss recognition]] and the
September 11th losses). Buffett, having just read *Jack: Straight from the Gut*, compared
Brandon to Jack Welch — "smart, energetic, hands-on, and expects much of both himself and his
organization" — and predicted he and Montross would make a disciplined General Re "a huge asset
for Berkshire."

Brandon led General Re's turnaround over the following years (it returned to consistent
underwriting profit), before departing in 2008 amid fallout from a finite-reinsurance legal
case; Montross succeeded him as CEO.

## Evolution
- [2001] Named General Re CEO to restore underwriting discipline; Buffett's "Jack Welch"
  comparison.
- [[2022]] **Back at Berkshire — captain of [[Alleghany Corporation|Alleghany]].** Fourteen years after
  leaving General Re, Brandon returns as CEO of the newly-acquired **[[Alleghany Corporation]]** (~$11.6B).
  Buffett: "I've worked with Joe in the past, and **he understands both Berkshire and insurance**." His
  return underscores Berkshire's "once in the family, always welcome" relationship with proven insurance
  operators.
