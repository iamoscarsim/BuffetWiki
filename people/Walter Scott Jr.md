# Walter Scott Jr.

Walter Scott Jr. is a longtime **[[Warren Buffett]]** friend and **Berkshire director** who
brought Berkshire its **first electric-utility investment**, **[[MidAmerican Energy]]**, in
1999. Chairman of Level 3 Communications and a substantial MidAmerican holder and board
member, Scott "casually asked" Buffett at a September 1999 California conference whether
Berkshire might invest — and "from the start the idea of being in partnership with Walter
struck me as a good one."

Scott "characteristically backed up his convictions with real money," adding to his and
his family's MidAmerican stake (to ~$280M) and becoming the utility's **controlling
shareholder** — "I can't think of a better person to hold that post." The partnership made
Berkshire's careful, PUHCA-constrained entry into utilities possible (see [[MidAmerican Energy]]) and set the stage for Berkshire's large later expansion in the field.
