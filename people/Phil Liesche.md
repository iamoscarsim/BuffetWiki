# Phil Liesche

Phil Liesche led the managerial group at [[National Indemnity Company]] and is
singled out in the [[1977]] letter for "an extraordinary underwriting
achievement" — large volume gains with excellent margins in NICO's traditional
auto and general liability lines, assisted by Roland Miller (underwriting) and
Bill Lyons (claims). He embodies the [[Insurance Underwriting Discipline]] Buffett
prizes: writing profitably and walking away from underpriced business.

## Evolution
- [1977] Praised for outstanding 1977 underwriting at National Indemnity.
- [1978–80] Repeated record underwriting profits while deliberately shrinking volume
  in soft markets; the embodiment of [[Insurance Underwriting Discipline]].
- [1982] **Retired at 65.** Buffett names him and predecessor [[Jack Ringwalt]] "the
  two prime movers in National Indemnity's success" — the operation "most important...
  in Berkshire's growth." Ran it as if he owned 100%.
