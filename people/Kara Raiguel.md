# Kara Raiguel

CEO of **[[General Re]]** from 2016, succeeding **[[Tad Montross]]** on his retirement after 39 years.
Buffett notes in the [[2016]] letter that Raiguel "has worked with **[[Ajit Jain]]** for 16 years" — a
pedigree from Berkshire's reinsurance powerhouse — and affirms she shares the underwriting discipline that
defines the franchise: like Montross, she will never accept the "the other guy is doing it, so we must as
well" excuse for sloppy underwriting (the fourth of the **four disciplines** — the willingness to walk
away). Her appointment continues Berkshire's pattern of deepening its insurance bench around Jain.

## Evolution
- [[2016]] Becomes CEO of General Re (from Tad Montross); 16 years working under Ajit Jain.
