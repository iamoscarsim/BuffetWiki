# Ted Weschler

The second of Berkshire's new-generation investment managers, who joined **shortly after year-end
2011** (following **[[Todd Combs]]**, who arrived at the start of 2011) ([[2011]]). A former
hedge-fund manager, Weschler famously won the seat in part through successive charity-auction lunches
with Buffett. Like Combs, he has "outstanding investment skills and a deep commitment to Berkshire"
and "the brains, judgment and character to manage our entire portfolio when Charlie and I are no
longer running Berkshire."

Each manager began with a few billion dollars (Weschler "will soon create" a ~$1.75B portfolio,
matching Combs), and each is paid **80% on his own results and 20% on his partner's** — a structure
built to reward individual success while fostering cooperation. Both are also expected to help the
next CEO evaluate acquisitions. Combs and Weschler together are Berkshire's designated post-Buffett
portfolio managers.

## Evolution
- [2011] Joins just after year-end as the second investment manager; ~$1.75B initial portfolio;
  80/20 own-vs-partner compensation; a future co-CIO with [[Todd Combs]].
- [2012]–[2013] Allocations steadily increased as both managers proved themselves.
