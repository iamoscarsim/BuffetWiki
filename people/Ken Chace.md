# Ken Chace

Ken Chace ran the [[Berkshire Hathaway Textiles]] operation. In the [[1977]]
letter Buffett credits his "energetic and straightforward" management and recalls
that Chace's efforts after the 1965 change in corporate control "generated capital
from the textile division needed to finance the acquisition and expansion of our
profitable insurance operation." Chace is thus, in a real sense, the man whose
textile cash flow seeded the [[National Indemnity Company]] purchase that made
Berkshire what it became.

## Evolution
- [1977] Praised for honest management of a structurally poor business; his
  textile cash funded the move into insurance.
- [1990] **Retires from Berkshire's board** at 75 (living in Maine). Buffett's tribute:
  Chace was "my immediate choice to run the textile operation after Buffett Partnership…
  assumed control… early in 1965. Although I made an economic mistake in sticking with
  the textile business, I made no mistake in choosing Ken: he ran the operation well, he
  was always 100% straight with me about its problems, and he generated the funds that
  allowed us to diversify into insurance." **Susan Buffett** is nominated to succeed him.
