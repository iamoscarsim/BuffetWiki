# Bob Wilmers

Bob Wilmers is the CEO of [[First Empire State]], the Buffalo-based bank holding
company. He is the reason Berkshire made an otherwise sub-scale investment: a $40M 9%
convertible preferred in 1991 ([[1991]]). Buffett wrote that he had "enormous respect
for Bob Wilmers" and "like[d] being his partner on any scale" — explicitly overriding
his usual minimum size for a position to back the man. Wilmers joins Buffett's roster
of admired bank managers ([[Carl Reichardt]] at [[Wells Fargo]]) whose quality, more
than the institution's headline economics, justified the commitment.

## Evolution
- [1991] Introduced as CEO of First Empire State; the reason for a deliberately small
  Berkshire preferred investment.
