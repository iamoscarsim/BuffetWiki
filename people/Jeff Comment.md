# Jeff Comment

Jeff Comment is the CEO of [[Helzberg's Diamond Shops]], hired by owner [[Barnett Helzberg]]
in 1988 (after serving as president of Wanamaker's) — a hire Buffett called "a homerun"
([[1995]]). His presence was the precondition for the deal: "We would not have bought the
business if Jeff had not been there to run it. Buying a retailer without good management is
like buying the Eiffel Tower without an elevator." Comment runs Helzberg's autonomously
within Berkshire, on the strong per-store productivity (~$2M average sales) that drives its
profits.

## Evolution
- [1995] Introduced as Helzberg's CEO (ex-Wanamaker's); the manager whose quality made the
  acquisition possible.
