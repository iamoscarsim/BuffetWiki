# Dan Burke

Dan Burke was the longtime number-two and then CEO of **[[Capital Cities]]/ABC**, the
operating partner of **[[Tom Murphy]]** in the management duo Buffett repeatedly held up as
the gold standard. In the [[1985]] letter (when Berkshire financed the ABC acquisition)
Buffett called Murphy and Burke the best management of any U.S. public company — "the sort of
fellows you'd want your daughter to marry" — and praised the pair as "stronger than the sum
of its parts" (the comparison he later applied to [[Carl Reichardt]] and Paul Hazen at
[[Wells Fargo]]).

In the [[1994]] letter Buffett thanks Burke on his **retirement** (alongside Reichardt of
Wells Fargo): both "encountered very tough industry conditions in recent years" yet brought
their companies through "with record earnings, added luster, and bright prospects," and
"prepared well for their departure," leaving their companies "in outstanding hands. We owe
them our gratitude."

## Evolution
- [1985] Introduced (with [[Tom Murphy]]) as half of the Cap Cities/ABC management Buffett
  most admired.
- [1994] **Retired** as CEO of Capital Cities/ABC; thanked for steering the company to record
  results through hard times and for orderly succession.
