# Ron Olson

Ronald Olson, a partner at **Munger, Tolles & Olson** ([[Charlie Munger]]'s law firm),
was [[Salomon]]'s lead lawyer on its government matters during the 1991 Treasury-auction
crisis. In the [[1992]] letter Buffett credits him as "key to our success in getting
through this trouble": with five federal authorities (the SEC, the New York Fed, the
Treasury, the U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York, and the DOJ Antitrust
Division) holding "important concerns about Salomon," resolving the crisis "in a
coordinated and prompt manner" required "a lawyer with exceptional legal, business and
human skills. Ron had them all." Olson would later join Berkshire's board of directors, a
relationship this page will track.
