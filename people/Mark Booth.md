# Mark Booth

The executive credited in the [[2006]] letter with turning **NetJets ([[Executive Jet]])** profitable
in **Europe** — the operation whose expansion (begun 1996) had run up a cumulative pre-tax loss of
$212 million by mid-2006. "Under Mark Booth's brilliant leadership, NetJets is now operating
profitably in Europe," with a net **589 customers added in 2005–06** as European demand "exploded" —
a key piece of NetJets' worldwide **$143M** pre-tax profit that year (alongside CEO Rich Santulli's
turnaround of the U.S. business).

## Evolution
- [2006] Leads NetJets Europe to profitability after years of losses; part of the NetJets turnaround.
