# Bill Kizer

Bill Kizer built [[Central States Indemnity]], the Omaha credit-card payment-protection
insurer Berkshire bought 82% of in December 1992, and stayed on to run it ([[1992]]). A
friend of Buffett's for over 35 years, Kizer — like [[Jack Ringwalt]] of [[National Indemnity Company]] before him — "thought of me when he wished to sell," the
owner-builder-to-Berkshire pattern Buffett prizes. His family (sons Bill, Dick, and John)
kept 18% of the company. Buffett: "We could not be associated with better people." In a
running joke about his sellers' fecundity, Buffett notes [[Frank Rooney]] (H. H. Brown)
has eight children and Kizer, "father of nine" — "It won't be easy to keep this string
going in 1993."
