# Bill Roberts

Became **CEO of [[GEICO]] on June 30, 2018**, succeeding **[[Tony Nicely]]** (who stayed on as Chairman).
Roberts was Nicely's "long-time partner" at the company; Buffett, who had "known and watched Bill operate
for several decades," judged the handoff another of Nicely's right calls ([[2018]]). He inherited
America's #2 auto insurer at the peak of the Nicely era — a low-cost franchise with ~$22B of float and a
$15.5B cumulative underwriting-profit record since Berkshire's 1996 buyout.
