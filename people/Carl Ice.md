# Carl Ice

Longtime **[[Burlington Northern Santa Fe|BNSF]]** executive who co-led the railroad with **[[Matt Rose]]**
(first noted in the [[2013]] letter) and oversaw its [[2015]] service turnaround. He served as CEO until
his **retirement at the end of 2020**, when his "number two," **[[Katie Farmer]]**, succeeded him. In the
pandemic year, Ice and Farmer "did an extraordinary job in controlling expenses," lifting BNSF's profit
margin **2.9 points despite a 7% volume decline** ([[2020]]).

## Evolution
- [2013]–[2015] Co-led BNSF with Matt Rose; central to the 2015 service-and-capex turnaround.
- [[2020]] Retired as CEO at year-end (succeeded by Katie Farmer) after a strong, cost-disciplined COVID year.
