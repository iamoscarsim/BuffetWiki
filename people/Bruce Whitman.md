# Bruce Whitman

A 43-year veteran of **[[FlightSafety International]]** who in 2004 succeeded founder **[[Al Ueltschi]]** as CEO ([[2004]]) — "(But Al's not going anywhere; I won't let him.)" Whitman shared
Ueltschi's safety-first creed that "flying an aircraft is a privilege to be extended only to people
who… are undeniably competent," and continued FlightSafety's standing as the world leader in pilot
training (283 simulators, $1.2B original cost; return on tangible equity up to 15.1% in 2004).
