# Bill Anders

Bill Anders — the former Apollo 8 astronaut — was CEO of [[General Dynamics]] when
Berkshire invested in 1992, and the reason a routine [[Arbitrage]] trade turned into a
long-term holding ([[1992]]). Buffett had ignored the company until its Dutch-tender
buyback drew him in for a quick profit; then, studying Anders' record, "what I saw made my
eyes pop." Anders "had a clearly articulated and rational strategy," pursued "with a sense
of urgency," producing "truly remarkable" results — sharply returning capital to
shareholders rather than empire-building in a post-Cold-War defense market with shrinking
demand. He is one of Buffett's exemplars of disciplined [[Capital Allocation]] in a
business that "must, or will" otherwise consume capital at low returns.

## Evolution
- [1992] Introduced as General Dynamics' CEO; his capital-return strategy converted
