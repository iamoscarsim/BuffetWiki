# Vance Bell

A 31-year veteran of **[[Shaw Industries]]** who became its CEO in 2006 ([[2006]]) when founder **Bob
Shaw** retired at 75. Shaw "recommended Vance Bell… and Bob, as usual, made the right call." Bell took
over the world's largest carpet maker just as the housing downturn began to slow the business, but
Shaw "remains a powerhouse and a major contributor to Berkshire's earnings."

## Evolution
- [2006] Succeeds founder Bob Shaw as CEO of Shaw Industries.
