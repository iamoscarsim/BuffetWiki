# Frank Ptak

CEO of the **[[Marmon Group]]** at its 2007 sale to Berkshire ([[2007]]). Ptak works closely with
long-time associate **John Nichols**, the former CEO of **Illinois Tool Works (ITW)**, where the two
had together run a mix of industrial businesses with a distinguished record. Buffett pointed
shareholders to "their ITW record; you'll be impressed." Ptak runs Marmon's 125 diverse industrial
businesses in the decentralized, hands-off style Berkshire prizes.

## Evolution
- [2007] Marmon's CEO at the Berkshire purchase; an ITW-pedigree industrial operator (with John
  Nichols).
