# Ted Seides

Co-manager of **Protégé Partners**, a fund-of-funds, and the only investment professional who accepted
Buffett's public wager that a low-cost S&P 500 index fund would, over ten years, beat any five
funds-of-funds a pro could select ([[Index Funds and Fees]]). For his side Seides
picked five funds-of-funds invested in more than 100 hedge funds. Buffett, who "hadn't known Ted before
our wager," praises him in [[2016]] as "straight-forward" and "meticulous," admiring his willingness "to
put his money where his mouth was" — even as the bet ran decisively against him (the funds-of-funds
averaging just 2.2% compounded over nine years vs. the index's 85.4% cumulative gain).
