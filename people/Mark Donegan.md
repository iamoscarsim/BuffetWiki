# Mark Donegan

CEO of **[[Precision Castparts]]**, the aerospace-components maker Berkshire agreed to buy for >$32B
in 2015 ([[2015]]). Buffett ranks him among his most admired operators, comparing him to **[[ISCAR| IMC]]'s Jacob Harpaz**: each "transform[s] very ordinary raw materials into extraordinary products
that are used by major manufacturers worldwide. Each is the **da Vinci of his craft**." A relentless
acquirer who built PCC into the world's premier supplier of aerospace castings and forgings, Donegan
was the kind of "cost-conscious and efficient" manager Berkshire prefers to buy and leave alone — and
Buffett looked forward to "hav[ing] him deploy Berkshire's capital."

## Evolution
- [2015] Introduced as PCC's CEO at the >$32B acquisition; the "da Vinci of his craft"; a serial
  acquirer to be backed with Berkshire's capital.
