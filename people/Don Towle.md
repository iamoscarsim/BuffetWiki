# Don Towle

Don Towle is the manager of **[[Kansas Bankers Surety]]**, the specialty insurer
Berkshire bought in 1996 and placed under [[Wesco Financial]]. Buffett calls him "an
extraordinary manager" who built KBS's "extraordinary underwriting record" through
first-hand relationships with hundreds of bankers and a command of "every detail of his
operation." He "thinks of himself as running a company that is 'his' — an attitude we
treasure at Berkshire" — the owner-operator mindset Buffett prizes across the
decentralized federation (see [[Decentralization]], [[Owner Orientation and Candor]]).

## Evolution
- [1996] Introduced as KBS's manager at acquisition; exemplifies the "runs it as if it's
  his own" temperament Berkshire looks for in the managers it inherits.
