# Jim Clayton

Founder of **[[Clayton Homes]]**, the manufactured-housing company Berkshire bought in 2003
([[2003]]). It was **Clayton's autobiography** — given to Buffett by University of Tennessee finance
students — that sparked the acquisition: Buffett "already knew the company to be the class act of the
manufactured housing industry" and admired Clayton's record of disciplined conduct in an industry
whose financing practices had grown "atrocious." By 2003 his son **[[Kevin Clayton]]** was running
the company as CEO. Buffett based his offer "**solely on Jim's book**, my evaluation of Kevin, the
public financials… and what I had learned from the Oakwood experience."
