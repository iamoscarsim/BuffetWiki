# Warren Buffett

Warren E. Buffett is Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway and the author of these
shareholder letters. The letters are written in the first person ("your
management") and serve simultaneously as an operating report, an investing
seminar, and a running record of his evolving philosophy — the subject this wiki
exists to track.

By the [[1977]] letter (signed March 14, 1978) his approach is already mature: a
relentless focus on **[[Return on Equity]]** over per-share headline growth; the
treatment of [[Stocks as Fractional Ownership]] of businesses; the four
**[[Acquisition Criteria]]**; insistence on businesses with structural tailwinds;
and candor about mistakes. His core collaborators and managers recur throughout
the letters: partner [[Charlie Munger]], and operating managers like
[[Phil Liesche]], [[Gene Abegg]], and [[Chuck Huggins]].
