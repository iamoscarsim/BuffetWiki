# Doris Christopher

Founder of **[[The Pampered Chef]]**, celebrated in the [[2002]] letter as a model
entrepreneur. A 34-year-old suburban-Chicago home-economics teacher "with a husband, two little
girls, and absolutely no business background," she borrowed **$3,000 against her life-insurance
policy** in 1980 — the only capital ever put into the company — and began selling kitchenware
through in-home demonstrations. Nearly talking herself out of her first presentation, she instead
sold $175 of goods and was underway; 22 years later the company did **>$700M** of sales through
67,000 consultants. Buffett: "Berkshire shareholders couldn't be luckier than to be associated
with Doris."
