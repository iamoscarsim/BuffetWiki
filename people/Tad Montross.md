# Tad Montross

Reinsurance executive who became **CEO of [[General Re]] in spring 2008**, succeeding **[[Joe Brandon]]**. Montross had been Brandon's partner in the multi-year campaign, begun in 2001, to
restore General Re's lost discipline in underwriting, reserving and expenses after the culture had
"deteriorated" (a problem Buffett "totally failed to detect" when Berkshire bought the reinsurer in
1998). By 2008 the turnaround was complete — "General Re has regained its luster" — and Buffett was
"certain that, with Tad, General Re's future is in the best of hands" ([[2008]]).

Under Montross, General Re cemented its standing as "the only reinsurer backed by an AAA corporation"
and, in Buffett's later phrase, the **"Tiffany of reinsurers"** restored to its factual underpinnings.

## Evolution
- [2001]–[2006] Brandon's partner in the General Re discipline-restoration effort (folded into the
  General Re page in those years).
- [2008] Named CEO of General Re as Joe Brandon stepped down; Buffett pronounces the turnaround
  complete.
- [[2016]] **Retired after 39 years** at General Re — "a class act in every way and we owe him a ton of
  thanks." Succeeded as CEO by **[[Kara Raiguel]]**.
