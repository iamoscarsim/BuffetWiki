# Melvyn Wolff

Melvyn Wolff is the longtime controlling shareholder and CEO of **[[Star Furniture]]**,
the Houston home-furnishings chain Berkshire acquired in 1997. The Wolff family's tie to
Star dates to 1924, but the business "struggled until Melvyn and his sister **Shirley
Toomin** took over in 1962" and built it into a 12-store Texas leader.

Buffett made the deal in "a single, two-hour session," needing only to know "I was dealing
with a man of integrity" — the owner-operator profile Berkshire prizes (see [[Acquisition Criteria]], [[Owner Orientation and Candor]]). The telling detail: on selling, Melvyn and
Shirley funded large special payments to *every* Star associate out of **their own**
proceeds — "Charlie and I love it when we become partners with people who behave like
that."

## Evolution
- [1997] Introduced as Star Furniture's CEO at acquisition (with sister Shirley Toomin);
