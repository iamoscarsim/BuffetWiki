# Byron Trott

The Goldman Sachs (later founder of BDT & Company) investment banker who became Buffett's most
trusted deal intermediary. First named in [[2003]] as the Goldman Managing Director who told Buffett
that Wal-Mart wished to sell **[[McLane]]** — his **third** Berkshire acquisition. Buffett's rare
praise of a banker: "He understands Berkshire far better than any investment banker with whom we
have talked and — **it hurts me to say this — earns his fee**."

Trott went on to broker further Berkshire deals (and, in [[2009]], the **Wm. Wrigley/Mars** financing
in which Berkshire participated). Buffett's repeated endorsement of Trott is notable precisely because
he is generally skeptical of the incentives of investment bankers in M&A (see [[Premium Takeovers]]).

## Evolution
- [2003] First named: sourced the McLane purchase from Wal-Mart; "the only investment banker who…
  earns his fee," already instrumental in three Berkshire acquisitions.
- [2009] Brokered Berkshire's role in the Mars/Wrigley financing; by now running his own merchant
  bank (BDT) and still Buffett's banker of choice.
