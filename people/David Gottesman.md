# David "Sandy" Gottesman

Founder of the investment firm **First Manhattan**, a decades-long friend of Buffett's and a major
long-term Berkshire shareholder, elected to the **Berkshire board in 2003** ([[2003]]) — one of four
owner-directors (with **Charlotte Guyman**, **[[Don Keough]]**, and **[[Tom Murphy]]**) added when
Berkshire reshaped its board around "true independence." Gottesman exemplifies the **owner-capitalism**
model Buffett prizes: a director whose family holds hundreds of millions of dollars of stock bought
in the market, so that "you win, they win big; you lose, they lose big."

## Evolution
- [2003] Joins the Berkshire board; longtime friend, First Manhattan founder, and large
  market-buyer of Berkshire stock — the owner-director ideal.
