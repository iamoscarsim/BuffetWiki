# Howard Buffett

Howard Buffett, Warren's son, **joined Berkshire's board in 1993** as part of Buffett's
succession planning ([[1993]]). He is explicitly *not* slated to manage the company; rather,
he and **[[Susan Buffett|Susie]]** are positioned to **represent the controlling ownership
interest** after Warren's death, when Berkshire moves into the third [[Corporate Governance]]
mode — a controlling owner separate from management. Buffett's design is that a non-executive
Buffett can safeguard Berkshire's culture and, if ever needed, change a CEO, without the
family running operations. (Howard is later designated as prospective **non-executive
chairman** — a development for future letters.)
