# Jim Kilts

The CEO who took over a struggling **[[Gillette]]** in 2001 and, in Buffett's [[2005]] telling,
"quickly instilled fiscal discipline, tightened operations and energized marketing, moves that
dramatically increased the intrinsic value of the company" — then engineered the **merger into
[[Procter & Gamble]]**. Buffett uses Kilts to make a point about CEO quality and pay: Gillette's
earlier **Duracell** acquisition had destroyed billions in shareholder value ("what Gillette received
in business value… was not equivalent to what it gave up"), whereas Kilts "**earned every penny**" of
his large compensation. "It's difficult to overpay the truly extraordinary CEO of a giant
enterprise. But this species is rare" — the rare counterpoint to Buffett's general critique of
[[Executive Compensation]].

## Evolution
- [2005] Praised as the rare CEO worth his pay — revived Gillette, then merged it into P&G; the
  positive bookend to the "Ratchet, Ratchet and Bingo" comp critique.
