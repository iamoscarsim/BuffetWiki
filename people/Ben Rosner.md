# Ben Rosner

Ben Rosner co-founded [[Associated Retail Stores]] in Chicago in 1931 and ran it
for decades, including after Berkshire acquired it via the [[Diversified Retailing Company]]
merger ([[1978]]). At 75 in the 1978 letter, he exemplifies the elderly
owner-operators Buffett treasures — alongside [[Gene Abegg]] (81) and Louie
Vincenti (73) — managers who "instinctively and unerringly think like owners." His
merchandising, real-estate, and cost-control skills produced ~20% after-tax
returns on capital in a no-growth business.

## Evolution
- [1978] Praised for outstanding profitability at Associated despite adverse
  retailing trends; emblem of the owner-minded manager.
- [1979–81] "Pulls rabbits out of the hat — big rabbits from a small hat"; earnings
  consistently realized in cash, not tied up in receivables/inventory.
- [1982] **Retired at 79.** Sold Associated for cash in 1967, "promised to stay on
  only until the end of the year, and then hit business home runs for us for the next
  fifteen years." Ran it as if he owned 100% — "their good character became our good
  fortune."
