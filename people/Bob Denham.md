# Bob Denham

Bob Denham was one of the [[Salomon]] executives Buffett credits with saving the firm
during and after the 1991 Treasury-auction scandal. In the [[1992]] letter Buffett names
him alongside Deryck Maughan, Don Howard, and John Macfarlane: "without [their] combined
efforts… the firm very probably would not have survived," calling them "tireless,
effective, supportive and selfless." Denham — who came from [[Charlie Munger]]'s law firm,
Munger, Tolles & Olson — became Salomon's chairman, helping steward Berkshire's preferred
investment through to its resolution.
