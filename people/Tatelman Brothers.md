# Tatelman Brothers

Barry and Eliot Tatelman are the brothers who built and ran **[[Jordan's Furniture]]**,
the New England retailer Berkshire acquired in 1999. Their grandfather began the business
in a Boston suburb in **1927**; under the brothers it became the largest furniture
retailer in both Massachusetts and New Hampshire, famous for its **"shoppertainment"**
approach and the **highest sales per square foot of any major furniture operation in the
country**.

Buffett calls them "classy people — just like their counterparts at Berkshire's three
other furniture operations." When they sold, they chose to give **every Jordan's employee
at least 50¢ for every hour he or she had ever worked** — a **$9 million** payment "from
the Tatelmans' own pockets, not from Berkshire's," and they "were thrilled to write the
checks." They are the fourth set of owner-operators in Berkshire's furniture group, after
the [[Nebraska Furniture Mart|Blumkins]], [[Bill Child]], and [[Melvyn Wolff]].
