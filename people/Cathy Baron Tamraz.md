# Cathy Baron Tamraz

President (and newly-named CEO) of **[[Business Wire]]** when Berkshire acquired it in 2005
([[2005]]). She sourced the deal herself: after a *Wall Street Journal* article on Berkshire's
acquisition practices, she wrote Buffett a two-page letter pitching Business Wire as a fit — winning
him with its description of a lean, cost-conscious, technology-investing culture ("We run a tight
ship… No secretaries or management layers here"). Buffett "felt Business Wire and Berkshire were a
fit" by the time he finished reading.
