# Phil Fisher

Philip Fisher was an influential growth-investing author (*Common Stocks and
Uncommon Profits*) whom Buffett cites as a shaping influence — one of the two
intellectual poles, alongside Benjamin Graham, of his investing philosophy. In the
[[1979]] letter Buffett invokes Fisher's analogy likening a corporation's pursuit
of shareholders to a restaurant attracting a matched clientele, to explain
[[Owner Orientation and Candor]] and his preference for stable, long-horizon
owners. Fisher's emphasis on business quality and durable growth complements the
[[Charlie Munger]]-driven move toward [[Good Business at a Fair Price]].
